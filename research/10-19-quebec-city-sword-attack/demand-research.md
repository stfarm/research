# Demand research — 10-19 Quebec City Halloween sword attack

Audience-interest signals only. Not fact sources.
View counts were scraped from YouTube search results on **2026-10-01**. Dates are YouTube relative ages as of that day. Views are not combined.

## Summary

- **Moderate demand, mainly news.** Global News' night-of clip has 243K; CBC victims tribute 89K. Few English creator long-forms exist (highest found under 20K).
- French-language coverage is deeper (Radio-Canada, TVA). TVA's explainer on the new trial has 11.5K.
- The March 2026 new-trial ruling is barely covered in English video. An accurate status update is a gap, and a Supreme Court decision (pending) would refresh interest.
