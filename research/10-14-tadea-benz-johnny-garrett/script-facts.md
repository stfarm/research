# Script facts — 10-14 Sister Tadea Benz / Johnny Frank Garrett

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.

# Essential facts

- **Sister Tadea Benz**, 76, a member of the Franciscan Sisters of Mary Immaculate, lived at St. Francis Convent in Amarillo, Texas. [10-14-S004, 10-14-S006]
- She was seen alive late on October 30, 1981. At about **7 a.m. on October 31**, another sister found her dead in her bed. [10-14-S001]
- The sister who found her did not suspect murder, and the body was taken to a funeral home. About an hour later the nuns found a broken window and called police. [10-14-S001]
- By the time investigators got the body back, it had been partly washed and embalmed. [10-14-S001]
- The autopsy found she had been raped and strangled. [10-14-S001]
- **Johnny Frank Garrett**, 17, who lived across the street, was arrested on November 9, 1981. [10-14-S006, 10-14-S011]

# Timeline

- 1982: convicted of capital murder in Amarillo and sentenced to death. [10-14-S006, 10-14-S009]
- 1984–1988: Texas and federal appeals courts uphold the conviction. [10-14-S001]
- Jan 1992: Sister Tadea's own community forgives him and asks the state to spare his life. Pope John Paul II asks for clemency. Gov. Ann Richards grants a 30-day reprieve. [10-14-S004, 10-14-S005]
- Feb 1992: the parole board votes 17-0 against clemency. [10-14-S005]
- **Feb 11, 1992**: executed at Huntsville, aged 28. [10-14-S005]
- 2004: his post-conviction lawyer, Jesse Quackenbush, asks for DNA testing and points to another man. [10-14-S008]
- 2008: Quackenbush releases the documentary *The Last Word*. [10-14-S009]

# Evidence reveal

- At trial: his fingerprints and palm prints were on a kitchen knife under her bed and on her headboard. [10-14-S001]
- He said he had burgled the convent two days earlier and touched the headboard reaching for a cross. A sister testified there was no cross above that bed. [10-14-S001]
- Police said he orally confessed but would not sign. He denied it. A jail inmate testified he admitted it. [10-14-S001]
- The pathologist, Dr. Ralph Erdmann, used up the entire biological sample and never tested for blood type. Nothing was left for DNA. [10-14-S001]
- In 1992, Erdmann pleaded no contest to falsifying autopsies in other cases. [10-14-S013]
- In 2005, a career offender, **Leoncio Perez Rueda**, pleaded guilty, after a DNA match, to raping and murdering 77-year-old Narnie Box Bryson in Amarillo in 1981. Garrett's lawyer claims Rueda described attacking a nun on Halloween night 1981. Rueda has never been charged in Sister Tadea's death. [10-14-S008, 10-14-S009, 10-14-S010]

# Ending

- Garrett was executed. His conviction stands. **No court has exonerated him and no DNA test has cleared him.** [10-14-S010, 10-14-S011]
- His sisters still campaign to clear his name. [10-14-S010]
- In 2005 the US Supreme Court barred executions for crimes committed under 18. [10-14-S017]
- Her community's words in 1992: they were "still convinced, ten years later, that faithfulness to Jesus Christ and to our founder, St. Francis, requires of us that we forgive Johnny Frank Garrett." [10-14-S004]

# Never say

- Never say DNA linked Rueda to Sister Tadea. No published test result shows this; the Benz sample was used up in 1981.
- Never say Garrett was exonerated, cleared, or proven innocent.
- Never say he was proven guilty beyond what the legal record shows. Say "convicted" and "executed".
- Never say Rueda killed Sister Tadea. Say Garrett's lawyer and family allege it.
- Never present the "curse" or the 2016 horror film as fact. The film is fiction.
- Never say Erdmann falsified this autopsy. His convictions were for other cases.
- Never say she was killed "on Halloween night". She was killed overnight on October 30–31 and found on Halloween morning.
- Never present a single last-words quote as official. Wording varies by outlet, and TDCJ's record differs.
- Never use the psychic/clairvoyant story without a primary source.
- Never describe the sexual assault in detail.
