# 10-09 — Rebekah Gay (Isabella County, Michigan, 2012)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01.

> **Minor privacy rule:** Rebekah's son was 3 years old in 2012. Some sources name him. **Do not name him.** Refer to him only as "her 3-year-old son".
>
> **Attribution rule:** The sexual-motive and necrophilia material comes from **police and court-filing statements of White's confession**. White pleaded guilty to **murder only**. No sexual offense was charged or adjudicated. Always attribute this material, and keep it to the minimum.
>
> **Suicide reporting rule:** White died in prison. State that he died in his cell and that the Michigan Department of Corrections reported it as suicide. **Do not describe the method.**

---

## Case summary

- Rebekah Jane Gay, 24, a young mother, was killed at her home on Coldwater Road in Isabella County, Michigan, in the early morning hours of **October 31, 2012**. [10-09-S001]
- John Douglas White, 55, a pastor of a small local church and a paroled ex-convict, was arraigned **November 1, 2012** on charges of first-degree murder and open murder. Police recovered Rebekah's body that morning. [10-09-S001, 10-09-S005]
- According to the sheriff and court filings, White confessed and led investigators to her body in a wooded area about half a mile from where she lived. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004]
- White had been released from prison in 2007 after serving nearly 12 years for manslaughter (a 1995 death in Kalamazoo County). He also had a 1981 conviction for choking and stabbing a 17-year-old girl. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004, 10-09-S006]
- He pleaded guilty on **March 27, 2013** to second-degree murder as a habitual third offender, with a stipulated minimum of at least 45.8 years. [10-09-S002]
- On **April 18, 2013**, Chief Judge Paul H. Chamberlain sentenced him to **56 years 3 months to 85 years**. [10-09-S002, 10-09-S006, 10-09-S007, 10-09-S008]
- White died in his cell at the Michigan Reformatory in Ionia on **August 28, 2013**. MDOC reported it as suicide. [10-09-S008]

## Victim profile

From her family obituary (Midland Daily News / Legacy.com) [10-09-S009], unless noted:

- **Rebekah Jane Gay**, born **March 26, 1988**, in Midland, Michigan. She lived in Mount Pleasant and was formerly of Midland.
- Graduated from Bullock Creek High School (2007) and In Session School of Cosmetology.
- Worked at BoRics Hair Care, then at the **Goodwill store in Mount Pleasant**, where she had recently been promoted to management.
- Described as "totally devoted to her son". Her family nickname was "Bekah-Boo".
- Engaged to Aaron Quinn.
- Survived by her parents, Thomas and Sally Gay; brothers James and Aaron; sister Deborah (Saunders).
- Her mother at sentencing: Rebekah was the family's "heart and soul". [10-09-S006]
- About 200 people gathered in her honor in November 2012 (CBS headline). [10-09-S014]

### Discrepancy notes

- **Plea date:** The official prosecutor release is dated **March 27, 2013** and says White "pled guilty today". The PDF filename reads 03292013. Earlier notes said March 28. **Use March 27, 2013.** [10-09-S002]
- **Sentence wording:** MLive: minimum 56 years 3 months. AP: "56 to 85 years". MidMichiganNow: 46-year minimum plus an additional 10 years. **Use "56 years 3 months to 85 years", or "at least 56 years".** [10-09-S006, 10-09-S007, 10-09-S008]
- **First alarm:** One account says she failed to show up for work at Goodwill and a co-worker checked her home (Oxygen, WPBN). Another, attributed to the sheriff via CBS Detroit, says she failed to pick up her son. Treat the exact sequence as unresolved. Safe phrasing: "When she didn't arrive at work, people began looking for her." [10-09-S003, 10-09-S010, 10-09-S011]
- **Location wording:** The prosecutor says "her home on Coldwater Road". WPBN says "M-20 residence near Coldwater Road". AP calls it a trailer in a mobile-home park. CBS (2013) says "Broomfield Township home". The prosecutor calls White "a Deerfield Township resident". **Use "her home in rural Isabella County, near Mount Pleasant"** and avoid naming a township for her home. [10-09-S001, 10-09-S005, 10-09-S006, 10-09-S010]
- **Relationship:** AP and CBS report White was **engaged to Rebekah's mother** and regularly babysat her son. One CBS Detroit summary describes him differently ("pastor at the victim's fiancé's church"). The obituary lists her parents as "Thomas and Sally Gay of Midland". **Do not name White's fiancée.** Use "engaged to Rebekah's mother", attributed to AP reporting. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004, 10-09-S005, 10-09-S009]

## Investigation

- **Discovery of disappearance:** She failed to arrive at work, and a co-worker went to her home. Her car was located behind The Barn Door bar. [10-09-S010, 10-09-S011]
- **Scene:** The sheriff said there were signs of "some sort of struggle". Her 3-year-old son had been left alone in the home. [10-09-S010, 10-09-S011]
- **Suspect focus:** White was a family acquaintance who babysat. Co-workers told reporters Rebekah had expressed discomfort around him. A resident told AP that White had scratches on his face. [10-09-S004, 10-09-S010]
- **Physical evidence (TV account):** Blood and a broken necklace in his truck bed, and a bag with a mallet, zip ties, garbage bags, and women's underwear. Medium-low confidence: this comes from the Oxygen program, not a court record. [10-09-S011]
- **Breakthrough:** According to the sheriff, White confessed after being told the body would deteriorate in the cold, wet weather, then led investigators to her body. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004]
- **Cause of death (offender confession, via sheriff):** Struck on the head with a mallet and strangled with a zip tie. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S005, 10-09-S010]

## Halloween connection

- The prosecutor's release places the killing "in the early morning hours of October 31, 2012". [10-09-S001]

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** On Halloween morning 2012, a young mother in rural Michigan didn't show up for work. The man her family trusted to babysit her son was a pastor, and a paroled killer. [10-09-S001, 10-09-S004]
- **Central question:** What happened to Rebekah between the night of October 30 and Halloween morning?
- **Turning point:** Within about 24 hours, White confessed and led investigators to her body. [10-09-S001, 10-09-S004]
- **Ending:** He pleaded guilty and received at least 56 years. Four months later he died in prison. Her sister called for a public violent-offender registry. [10-09-S006, 10-09-S008]
