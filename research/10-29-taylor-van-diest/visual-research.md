## 2026-10-10 refresh

### Identification checks

- Costume photos: use only those published by RCMP/CBC in Nov 2011 (10-11-S012). Many creator thumbnails use generic zombie-makeup stock — **not Taylor**.
- Taylor has a **twin sister, Kirstie**. Photos of "Taylor" may show Kirstie; use only captioned images from police/family releases.
- Foerster: use 2012 arrest/2014 court images from Castanet/Global only.

### Historical reference vs later recreation

- **Historical (licensed only):** RCMP costume photos; Castanet 2011–2018 news footage; 2026 trail clean-up photos (Black Press).
- **Recreation (illustrative):** dusk railway tracks behind a small-town street; a phone screen with a text bubble (do not reproduce the real message screen as if it were evidence — label as dramatization); search party flashlights along rails.

### Thumbnail concepts (10-09 brand standard)

1. **"Last text"** — young adult woman in subtle zombie makeup (illustrative, not a likeness), lit by her phone screen at dusk on railway tracks, a figure far behind out of focus. White: `TAYLOR VAN DIEST`. Gold: `HER LAST TEXT: "BEING CREEPED"`.
2. **"Shortcut"** — low angle down the rails at blue hour, costumed silhouette walking away, flashlight beam behind her. Gold: `SHE TOOK A SHORTCUT ON HALLOWEEN`.
3. **"Fake ID"** — older man's hands sliding an ID card across a table (illustrative). Gold: `HIS FATHER HELPED HIM HIDE`.

No depiction of the attack, flashlight or shoelace.
