## Case summary

- On **Halloween night, October 31, 2009**, three teenage girls (16, 17 and 17) were walking home after a birthday party and trick-or-treating in **Dale City**, Prince William County, Virginia. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S007]
- Near **Glendale Plaza (4300 block of Dale Boulevard)**, a masked man approached them, asked for money, and forced them at what appeared to be gunpoint into the woods behind the shopping centre. He sexually assaulted the two 17-year-olds. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S007]
- The 16-year-old **secretly texted her mother and friends** to call 911. Hearing sirens, the attacker fled. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S009]
- DNA from the scene matched a profile linked to a string of attacks on women since 1997 in Maryland, Virginia, Connecticut and Rhode Island, which investigators called the **"East Coast Rapist"**. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- In **February 2011**, a billboard and website campaign produced a tip naming **Aaron H. Thomas**. Police collected a cigarette he threw away; its DNA matched. He was arrested in New Haven, Connecticut, on **March 4, 2011**. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- He pleaded guilty in Virginia in 2012 and was sentenced to life terms in 2013. He later pleaded guilty to three Maryland rapes (2015). [10-16-S007, 10-16-S008, 10-16-S010]

## Victim profile

- **Three girls, aged 16, 17 and 17**, from the Dale City area. They had been at a birthday party and then went trick-or-treating. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S007]
- At sentencing in 2013, two of the survivors said they had forgiven Thomas; one said he should never be released. [10-16-S008]

## Investigation

- **The text message:** The youngest girl's text to her mother brought police while the attack was still under way, and the sirens drove him off. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S009]
- **DNA:** Scene DNA tied Halloween 2009 to an existing interstate series. The DNA did not identify him because his profile was not in any database. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- **Elimination work:** Investigators ruled out more than 700 men. [10-16-S002]
- **Breakthrough (publicity):** The February 2011 billboard and website campaign, with FBI involvement, produced the tip within days. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002, 10-16-S003]
- **Abandoned DNA:** Officers followed him and collected a cigarette butt he discarded; the lab matched it. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- **Confession:** A detective testified he admitted the Dale City attack. [10-16-S005]
- **Failure / gap:** The series ran 12 years without an identification because his DNA was never in a database. Sources reviewed do not allege police error.
