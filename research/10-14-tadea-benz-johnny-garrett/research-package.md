# 10-14 — Sister Tadea Benz / Johnny Frank Garrett (Amarillo, Texas, 1981)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-01.

> ## LEGAL STATUS — READ FIRST
> - **Conviction:** Johnny Frank Garrett was convicted of capital murder (1982) and **executed by Texas on February 11, 1992**. Every appeal failed. [10-14-S001, 10-14-S005]
> - **No exoneration:** No court has vacated the conviction or declared him innocent. No DNA test result clearing him has been published. [10-14-S010, 10-14-S011]
> - **Doubts are real but unproven:** a documentary (2008), his family, and his post-conviction lawyer argue another man, **Leoncio Perez Rueda**, may have been responsible. That is an **allegation/theory**, not a finding. [10-14-S008, 10-14-S009, 10-14-S010]
> - Present both the legal record and the doubts. Do not declare him guilty beyond the record or innocent.

## Case summary

- **Sister Tadea Benz**, 76, a Franciscan nun, was found dead in her second-floor bedroom at **St. Francis Convent**, Amarillo, at about **7:00 a.m. on October 31, 1981**. She had been seen alive late the previous evening. [10-14-S001]
- The pathologist found she had been raped and died of **manual strangulation**. [10-14-S001]
- **Johnny Frank Garrett**, 17, who lived across the street, was arrested on **November 9, 1981**. [10-14-S006, 10-14-S011]
- A jury convicted him of capital murder in 1982 and he was sentenced to death. Fingerprints and palm prints on a knife under the bed and on the bed's headboard matched his. [10-14-S001, 10-14-S002]
- Despite appeals from Pope John Paul II, Texas Catholic bishops, and the nun's own religious community, he was executed on **February 11, 1992**, aged 28. [10-14-S004, 10-14-S005]
- Since 2004, his family and lawyers have argued he was wrongly convicted. No court has agreed. [10-14-S008, 10-14-S010]

## Victim profile

- **Sister Tadea Benz**, 76, member of the **Franciscan Sisters of Mary Immaculate**, living at St. Francis Convent, Amarillo. [10-14-S004, 10-14-S006]
- In January 1992, her community said it was "still convinced, ten years later, that faithfulness to Jesus Christ and to our founder, St. Francis, requires of us that we forgive Johnny Frank Garrett", and, "as the family of Sister Tadea Benz", asked for his sentence to be commuted. [10-14-S004]
- **Research gap:** no reliable source found for her birthplace, years in religious life, or work. Contact the Franciscan Sisters of Mary Immaculate (Amarillo) before production for a respectful biography and photo permissions.

## Investigation

- **Discovery:** The nun who found Sister Tadea saw blood on her face but did not suspect murder. The body went to a funeral home before police were called. [10-14-S001]
- **Investigative failure 1 (scene/body):** By the time investigators recovered the body, it had been partly cleansed and arterially embalmed. [10-14-S001]
- **Investigative failure 2 (biological evidence):** Dr. Ralph Erdmann used the entire vaginal-wash sample for sperm and prostate-secretion tests and did **not** test for blood type. Nothing was left for the defence to test. The Fifth Circuit held this was not unconstitutional destruction of evidence. [10-14-S001]
- **Erdmann's later record:** In 1992 he pleaded no contest to seven felony counts of falsifying autopsies in other cases. **No court has found misconduct in the Benz autopsy.** [10-14-S013]
- **Suspect:** Garrett lived across the street (with his mother). A witness saw him running from the direction of the convent that night. [10-14-S001, 10-14-S006]
- **Statement:** Two officers testified he orally admitted the break-in and attack and agreed a written version was true but would not sign it before seeing a lawyer. He denied making it. [10-14-S001]
- **Informant:** Jail trusty Lonnie Watley testified Garrett eventually admitted the killing to him. [10-14-S001]
- **Alternative suspect (later):** Leoncio Perez Rueda. See "Later doubts".
- **Psychic claim:** Advocates say police drew on a clairvoyant. **Not verified in any primary source.** Do not use without one. [10-14-S016]

## Halloween connection

**Classification: C** (major discovery on October 31), with A likely but unconfirmed.

- Her body was found at about 7 a.m. on **October 31, 1981**. [10-14-S001]
- She was last seen late on October 30, so she was killed overnight. The court record does not fix whether it was before or after midnight. Prosecution-based summaries say "the morning of October 31"; UPI wrote "Halloween night", which is inaccurate. [10-14-S001, 10-14-S005, 10-14-S011]
- Say "killed overnight and found on Halloween morning". No Halloween activity is involved.

### Last statement discrepancy

UPI and NYT report versions of a statement thanking his family and telling "the rest of the world" to "kiss my ass" (wording differs between outlets). Wikipedia says TDCJ's official record lists no statement. Treat the exact wording as **reported, not official**. [10-14-S005, 10-14-S007, 10-14-S011]

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** On Halloween morning 1981, a 76-year-old nun is found dead in her convent bed, and the first person to find her does not realise she was murdered. By the time police are called, her body is already at a funeral home. [10-14-S001]
- **Central question:** Did Texas execute the right man?
- **Investigation turning point:** Garrett's fingerprints on the knife and headboard against a lost biological sample, an unsigned statement, and an informant. Then, 23 years later, a career offender is matched by DNA to a similar 1981 Amarillo murder. [10-14-S001, 10-14-S009]
- **Ending:** Executed in 1992 despite the Pope, the bishops, and the victim's own sisters asking for mercy. Doubts raised since 2004 have never been tested in court. [10-14-S004, 10-14-S005, 10-14-S010]
