# 10-20 — Manfred and Marísia von Richthofen (São Paulo, Brazil, 2002)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-01.

> ## READ FIRST
> - **All three were convicted** by a jury in July 2006 of the double murder: **Suzane von Richthofen** (the couple's daughter), **Daniel Cravinhos** (her boyfriend) and **Cristian Cravinhos** (his brother). [10-20-S002, 10-20-S007]
> - **None is in a closed prison today.** All three serve the remainder of their sentences in the **open regime** (*regime aberto*): living outside prison under court conditions. This is **not** a pardon, acquittal, or end of sentence. [10-20-S004, 10-20-S005]
> - Many dramatizations exist. **Do not use them as evidence.** Different films present Suzane's and Daniel's competing versions. [10-20-S010]

## Case summary

- In the early hours of **October 31, 2002**, engineer **Manfred Albert von Richthofen**, 49, and psychiatrist **Marísia von Richthofen**, 50, were beaten to death in their beds at the family home in **Campo Belo**, South Zone of São Paulo. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- Their daughter **Suzane**, 19, let her boyfriend **Daniel Cravinhos** and his brother **Cristian** into the house; the brothers carried out the killings. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- The three staged a burglary, then established alibis: Suzane and Daniel at a motel, Cristian elsewhere. Suzane and her 15-year-old brother **Andreas** "discovered" the scene and police were called. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- Investigators noticed no forced entry, a disabled alarm, an odd pattern of disturbance, and Suzane's demeanour. Cristian had just bought a motorcycle with US dollars. All three **confessed on November 8, 2002**. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- In **July 2006**, a jury convicted all three. Suzane and Daniel received **39 years 6 months**; Cristian **38 years 6 months**. [10-20-S002, 10-20-S007]
- After moving through the regime system, all three now live outside prison in the open regime (Daniel since 2018, Suzane since January 2023, Cristian since March 2025). [10-20-S004, 10-20-S005]

## Victim profile

- **Manfred Albert von Richthofen** (born Feb 3, 1953), 49. German-born engineer; a director at **DERSA**, São Paulo's state highway company. [10-20-S002, 10-20-S003]
- **Marísia von Richthofen** (born Jan 19, 1952), 50. Psychiatrist. [10-20-S002, 10-20-S003]
- Children: **Suzane** (19, law student, per wide reporting) and **Andreas** (15). Andreas was not involved. [10-20-S001]
- The parents opposed Suzane's relationship with Daniel. [10-20-S001]
- In 2011 a court declared Suzane "unworthy" to inherit (*indignidade*); the estate passed to Andreas. [10-20-S002]
- **Research gap:** family-approved biographical material about Manfred and Marísia is scarce; Andreas has avoided the press. Do not approach or depict him beyond public-record facts.

## Investigation

- **Discovery:** Reported as a burglary by Suzane and Andreas in the early hours of Oct 31. [10-20-S001]
- **Staged scene:** The library was ransacked while other rooms were orderly; jewellery and a gun were left; money was taken from a briefcase. Police viewed it as an amateur staging. [10-20-S002]
- **Physical clues:** No forced entry; alarm disabled — pointing to someone with access. [10-20-S001]
- **Initial suspect:** a former employee, ruled out. [10-20-S001]
- **Breakthrough:** Cristian's purchase of a motorcycle with US dollars, in another person's name, days after the killings. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- **Behaviour:** Officers noted Suzane's calm and her questions about the money; this was reported widely but is **observational, not evidence of guilt by itself**. [10-20-S002]
- **Confessions:** After repeated statements, all three confessed (Nov 8, 2002). [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]

## Halloween connection

**Classification: A + C.**

- **A:** The killings took place in the early hours of **October 31, 2002**. [10-20-S001]
- **C:** The bodies were discovered and police called around 4 a.m. the same day. [10-20-S002]
- **Not B:** No Halloween activity was involved. Halloween is a minor holiday in Brazil; the date is coincidental. Creator titles calling it "The Halloween Horror" overstate the connection.

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** At 4 a.m. on October 31, 2002, a 19-year-old law student and her younger brother call police to report a burglary at their parents' São Paulo home. Within nine days she has confessed to planning it. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- **Central question:** Who killed Manfred and Marísia, and why did the burglary look so wrong?
- **Investigation turning point:** A motorcycle bought with US dollars. [10-20-S001]
- **Ending:** Convictions in 2006; by 2025 all three were living outside prison in the open regime. The surviving son inherited the estate. [10-20-S002, 10-20-S004]
