# Source log — 10-27 Chris Jenkins (Minneapolis, Minnesota, Halloween 2002)

Searched: **2026-10-11**. Latest **official** status statement found: Minneapolis Police Department, **Apr 29, 2008** (JENK-S005). Latest secondary status report: Nov 2022 (JENK-S008, relaying a 2018 TV episode and the family's 2018 comments). Source IDs are case-keyed (`JENK-`).

## Access notes

- No court record (no charge). The 2007 county-attorney charging review is known only from secondary sources (see contradictions).
- Star Tribune and WCCO archives not searched successfully; Journal Times (AP) 2006 article rendered without body text.
- NBC's "Investigator's notes" page is a redacted private-investigator log published by NBC (Dateline) — a primary document from the family's investigator, not a police record.
