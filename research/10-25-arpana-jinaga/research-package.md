# 10-25 — Arpana Jinaga (Redmond, Washington, Halloween 2008)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-10.

> ## READ FIRST — HIGH LEGAL RISK
> - **Unsolved.** The only person charged, **Emanuel Fair, was acquitted** (June 11, 2019) after ~9 years in jail. He sued King County, the City of Redmond and others (*Fair v. King County*, W.D. Wash. 2:21-cv-01706). That civil case went to a **jury trial in late Sept 2026; the docket shows a jury verdict on Oct 1 and a judgment on Oct 2, 2026 — the outcome could not be read (PACER documents not accessible) and no news report was found.** Do not state who won. Present him as acquitted; never imply guilt. [10-25-S001, 10-25-S002, 10-25-S007]
> - **Never name the next-door neighbour** who was the early suspect and received immunity. Never name any party guest as a suspect.
> - She was **raped and strangled**; keep the sexual violence and the evidence-contamination details non-graphic.
> - Halloween fit: she **co-hosted** the building's Halloween party on Oct 31, 2008, and was killed hours later (investigators: ~8 a.m. Nov 1). Class **B**. [10-25-S002]

## Halloween connection

**Classification: B (+ A for the party date).** Arpana and other tenants co-hosted a large Halloween party on Oct 31, 2008; she returned to her apartment ~3 a.m. and was killed hours later. [10-25-S002, 10-25-S001] This is now also an **admitted fact in federal court**: she was murdered at the Valley View Apartments "sometime in the morning hours of November 1, 2008, after she hosted a Halloween party with other residents of her apartment complex on the night of October 31, 2008." [10-25-S008]

- Costume (Little Red Riding Hood) and a "haunted forest" apartment theme: **magazine only** (S006). Treat as attributed until a second source confirms.

## Investigation and evidence (as reported)

- Prosecutors' DNA: Fair's DNA on her neck, on duct tape used as a gag, and with her blood on a robe from the building dumpster (probabilistic genotyping; state lab had called mixtures too complex). [10-25-S001, 10-25-S002, 10-25-S003]
- Defence: dumpster security/tampering; DNA of **another man** (not at the party) on the bootlace likely used; the neighbour's undisclosed 3 a.m. calls and attempted border crossing; immunity deal. [10-25-S001, 10-25-S002]
- Jury acquitted. Prosecutors were barred from arguing an accomplice theory. [10-25-S004]

## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks:**
1. She helped throw her building's Halloween party. At 3 a.m. she went home to bed. By morning someone had kicked in her door. [S002]
2. Her killer spent hours trying to erase himself — and the case still has no killer. [S001, S002]
3. The man charged spent nine years in jail. A jury set him free. [S001, S002]

**Suspense beats:** party photos → last sighting 3 a.m. → silence for two days → family in India calls a friend → door kicked in → a scene scrubbed with cleaner and oil → a guest charged → DNA software fight → hung jury → acquittal → lawsuit.

**Reveal / question:** The forensic case collapsed in front of a jury; the evidence trail points in more than one direction; Arpana's killer has never been convicted.

## Distinguishing details

- Victim co-hosted the party (S002).
- Elaborate evidence contamination (S002).
- Probabilistic DNA software at the centre of the trial (S003).
- Nine years in jail without conviction; civil-rights suit tried to verdict in Oct 2026 (outcome unknown) (S002, S005, S009).

## Disputed claims / myths / avoid

- Do not name the neighbour or the "other man" whose DNA was on the bootlace.
- Do not frame Fair as "got away with it".
- Do not show party photos with identifiable guests.
