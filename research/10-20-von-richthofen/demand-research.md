# Demand research — 10-20 Manfred and Marísia von Richthofen

Audience-interest signals only. Not fact sources.
View counts were scraped from YouTube search results on **2026-10-01**. Dates are YouTube relative ages as of that day. Views are not combined.

## Summary

- **Very strong demand**, in both English and Portuguese. Coffeehouse Crime's English video has 3.9M views; the archival TV interview clip has 3.9M; Portuguese documentaries exceed 2M.
- English coverage is heavily framed around Suzane ("Self-Entitled Teen", "Evil Blonde"). The victims are rarely centred.
- The 2025 series *Tremembé* (Prime Video) renewed interest. Most creator videos predate Cristian's 2025 open-regime move.
