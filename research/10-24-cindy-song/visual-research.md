## D. Thumbnail concepts (10-09 brand standard; no sexualisation)

1. **"She made it home"** — young East Asian woman (illustrative, not a likeness) with bunny ears and a red hooded coat, seen from behind climbing an outdoor apartment stairway at 4 a.m. under a sodium light. White: `CINDY SONG`. Gold: `SHE MADE IT HOME. THEN SHE VANISHED`.
2. **"What she left behind"** — close-up of a phone, a backpack strap and a pair of false eyelashes on a dresser; bunny ears absent (sourced items). Gold: `HER PHONE STAYED. SHE DIDN'T`.
3. **"After Halloween"** — a missing-poster style frame on a lamppost with a costume headband lying in leaves below. Gold: `GONE HOURS AFTER HALLOWEEN NIGHT`.

Rules: no peril depiction, no abduction imagery (not established), no "Playboy" wording.
