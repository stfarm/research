### PH-1. Missing sabungeros
- **Facts:** Dozens of men tied to online cockfighting (e-sabong) vanished from 2021. In 2025 a whistleblower, Julie "Dondon" Patidongan, alleged 100+ were killed and dumped in Taal Lake. Roughly 887 bone fragments have been retrieved; reporting says no positive DNA identification yet.
- **Allegations (not proven):** Patidongan named businessman Atong Ang and actress Gretchen Barretto; both deny it. The DOJ murder complaint was submitted for resolution.
- **Twists:** Whistleblower account; lake recovery; relatives denying a settlement (Jul 2026).
- **Hook:** "Over 100 men may be at the bottom of a volcanic lake. A cockfighting empire says it knows nothing."
- **Flag:** Ongoing. Named living persons. Use "alleged" and "denied" every time.
- **Sources:** [Tribune: new witness](https://tribune.net.ph/2025/07/12/sabungeros-case-new-witness-surfaces) · [Tribune: DOJ bones](https://tribune.net.ph/2025/09/09/doj-more-bones-retrieved-from-taal-lake) · [Philstar Nov 2025](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2025/11/20/2488724/missing-sabungeros-search-57-more-skeletal-remains-found-taal-lake) · [MB Jul 2026](https://mb.com.ph/2026/07/18/two-relatives-of-a-missing-sabungero-deny-out-of-court-settlement-with-atong-ang) · [GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/963193/missing-sabungeros-taal-lake/story/)

### PH-3. Vizconde massacre
- **Facts:** June 1991, Parañaque: Estrellita Vizconde and daughters Carmela and Jennifer killed. Hubert Webb and others were convicted at trial and by the Court of Appeals (2005, life). On 14 Dec 2010 the Supreme Court acquitted Webb and six co-accused 7-4, finding the star witness Jessica Alfaro not credible and Webb's US-alibi proven. A DNA test request failed because the specimen could not be located. Prescription lapsed in 2011.
- **Disputed:** Vizconde family and others rejected the acquittal; lobbying allegations were denied by justices.
- **Hook:** "20 years of prison, then the Supreme Court said: wrong men. So who did it?"
- **Flag:** Disputed. Sexual violence involved; keep non-graphic.

## 7. Regional audience-growth experiment

**Question:** Do locally localized Philippine cases (Taglish hook plus Tagalog on-screen captions) convert viewers into subscribers more efficiently per 1,000 views than the same format in English-only?

**Design**
- **Arm A (Taglish):** 4 PH cases with English narration, Tagalog/Taglish on-screen hook (first 3 seconds) and captions.
- **Arm B (English control):** 4 matched PH cases, English-only.
- **Arm C (Regional benchmark):** 2 non-PH cases (Mona Fandey, Am Cyanide), English-only, to measure international pull.
- Match cases across arms by score; alternate slots so day-of-week is balanced. Same length (60-110 s), same style, same posting time.
- Do not post the same story twice with two languages; duplicate content gets suppressed.

**Metrics (YouTube Analytics / TikTok / Meta Insights)**

| Metric | Measure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Geography | % of views from PH, US, UK, CA, AU, other | Analytics > Geography |
| Retention | Viewed-vs-swiped-away %, average % viewed, 3-sec and 10-sec hold | Shorts analytics |
| Shares | Shares per 1,000 views | Analytics |
| Subscribers | Subscribers gained per 1,000 views | Analytics, by video |
| Returning viewers | Share of views from returning vs new | Audience tab |

**Run window:** 14 days per video, read at day 7 and day 14. Total experiment: 10 videos over the November slate.

**Decision rule (starting thresholds; recalibrate them to your own trailing-90-day channel median once you have it, because I have no baseline numbers):**
- Scale Arm A if its median subscribers per 1,000 views beats Arm B by ≥25%, **and** PH share of views is ≥30% higher, **and** average % viewed is not lower than Arm B.
- Scale non-PH if Arm C subscribers per 1,000 views beats both PH arms.
- Treat any result as inconclusive if fewer than 5,000 views per video; extend the test by 2 weeks.
- With 10 videos this is a directional test, not statistically conclusive. Report medians and ranges, not averages.
