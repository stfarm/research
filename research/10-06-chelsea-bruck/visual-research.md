# 10-06 — Chelsea Bruck
## Visual research

**Observation date: 30 September 2026.**

**Method and limitation.** No image was downloaded or reproduced. Direct page
retrieval was blocked by the session's network policy, so entries were
identified through search — usually from the article's own text describing what
it showed. **No image was visually inspected.** Where an entry says what an
image depicts, that is from the source's description, not from viewing it.

**Rights posture:** nothing here is cleared. Because this is a 2014–2017 case,
most usable imagery is **broadcast/news photography (copyrighted)** or
**family-supplied portraits released for a missing-person appeal** — the latter
deserve particular restraint: they were circulated to find her, not to
illustrate entertainment.

**Editorial note carried over from the research package:** the Bruck family
publicly asked for sensitivity during the search, saying "This isn't a detective
show" [10-06-S35]. That should govern how her images are used.

- **Entries catalogued: 26** (target 15–25 — slightly over; every entry ties to
  a verified timeline beat).
- **Government/official-origin candidates: 4** (V01, V08 booking, V14, V15) —
  the missing-person poster and the composite sketch are the two strongest
  reusable prospects, if sourced from the Monroe County Sheriff's Office
  directly rather than lifted from news pages.

- **Nothing was downloaded. Nothing here is cleared.**

### Gaps worth chasing on an unrestricted connection
1. **Monroe County Sheriff's Office** — request the original missing-person
   poster and composite sketch as official releases. Cleanest rights position
   available in this case.
2. **Monroe County Community College** — permission for a scholarship/portrait
   image for the closing beat.
3. **The Monroe News** (Monroe County's local newspaper) was not reachable in
   this session and is almost certainly the deepest photographic archive on
   this case, including trial photography and anniversary coverage.
4. **Michigan Court of Appeals opinion PDF** — not an image source, but its
   factual recitation should anchor any on-screen text cards.
5. A **court-released exhibit list** would establish exactly what costume
   imagery entered evidence — relevant before depicting the costume at all.
