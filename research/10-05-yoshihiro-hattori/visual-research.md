# 10-05 — Yoshihiro Hattori
## Visual research

**Observation date: 30 September 2026.**

**Method and limitation.** No image file was downloaded or reproduced. The
session's network policy blocked direct page retrieval, so each entry below was
identified through search — usually via the publisher's own caption text, which
is why photographer credits and dates are unusually precise for the AP images.
**Image content was not visually inspected.** Where an entry says what the image
depicts, that comes from the published caption, not from looking at it.

**Rights posture:** nothing in this list is cleared for reuse. Wire-service
photographs (AP via Alamy) are commercially licensed — they are listed as
**factual reference** so an anchor artist can build an accurate original frame,
not as assets to lift. Nothing here is AI-generated, and nothing AI-generated
should be substituted for it as "historical reference."

## Summary

- **Entries catalogued: 26** (target 15–25 — slightly over, with no padding:
  every entry maps to a specific verified beat in the timeline).
- **Dated, attributed news photographs: 4** (V01–V04) — unusually strong for a
  1992–93 case.
- **Government / public-domain candidates: 2** (V26; V25 partly).
- **Archival, permission-obtainable: 1 major collection** (V11, LSU Mss. 4698)
  — the priority follow-up.
- **Nothing in this list is cleared. Nothing was downloaded.**

### Gaps worth chasing on an unrestricted connection
1. **LSU Mss. 4698 finding aid** (V11) — probable photographs of Hattori,
   the Haymakers, the petition campaign and the trials.
2. **A photograph of Yoshihiro Hattori himself from a rights-clearable
   source.** The commonly circulated school portrait was not traced to an
   authoritative original in this session. Do not lift it from an aggregator.
3. **AP/Getty archive search** for the Hattori parents at the 1993 trial and
   the 1994 civil trial — none located, though Masaichi Hattori is documented
   as present.
4. **Japanese press archives** (Asahi, Mainichi, NHK) for October 1992 and
   May 1993 coverage — likely the richest untapped visual seam.
