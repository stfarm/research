# 10-21 — Shauna Howe (Oil City, Pennsylvania, 1992)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-01.

> ## READ FIRST
> - **Halloween was not cancelled.** After Shauna's murder, Oil City moved trick-or-treating to **daylight hours**. Evening trick-or-treating returned in **2008** after a 10-year-old's petition. [10-21-S003, 10-21-S009]
> - **Convictions:** brothers **James E. O'Brien** and **Timothy M. O'Brien** (jury, Oct 2005; life + 15–30 years). **Eldred "Ted" Walker** pleaded guilty (2005) to third-degree murder and kidnapping; he died in prison in 2022. [10-21-S004, 10-21-S005]

## Case summary

- On **October 27, 1992**, **Shauna Howe**, 11, was walking home from a **Girl Scout Halloween party** in Oil City, Pennsylvania, at about 8 p.m. [10-21-S006, 10-21-S007]
- Two blocks from home, at **West First and Reed streets**, a man grabbed her; a witness saw it. [10-21-S007, 10-21-S008]
- Her body was found on **October 30, 1992**, at **Coulter's Hole** in Rockland Township, beneath a railroad trestle about eight miles away. [10-21-S003, 10-21-S005]
- The case went unsolved for nearly ten years. Preserved biological evidence was later matched to **James O'Brien** by DNA (link reported in 2002). [10-21-S007, 10-21-S008]
- James and his brother **Timothy** were arrested in July 2004 and convicted in October 2005. A third man, **Ted Walker**, pleaded guilty and testified against them. [10-21-S001, 10-21-S004, 10-21-S005]

## Victim profile

- **Shauna Howe**, born July 11, 1981; 11 years old. [10-21-S008]
- A Girl Scout. At sentencing the judge noted her troop's work visiting senior citizens at a convalescent home. [10-21-S004]
- Her favourite colour was **purple**; the 30-year memorial walk asked people to bring purple painted stones. [10-21-S006]
- Family: mother **Lucy Brown**; sisters **Emily Krebs** and **Jennifer Makin**. [10-21-S005, 10-21-S006]
- Lucy Brown (2022): "There are kids out there running around after dark. I want people to remember Shauna." [10-21-S006]

## Investigation

- **Witness:** Someone saw the grab at First and Reed and described a tall, thin man. [10-21-S007]
- **Evidence preservation:** Investigators kept biological evidence from Shauna's body and costume. This made the 2002 DNA match possible. [10-21-S007, 10-21-S008]
- **DNA breakthrough:** DNA matched **James O'Brien**. Reports say his sample was on file from an unrelated matter; **the exact origin and year of his sample is not confirmed** in sources reviewed. [10-21-S007]
- **Second witness:** In 2002 a witness recognised Walker on television. [10-21-S007]
- **Walker's testimony:** He grabbed Shauna and handed her to Timothy O'Brien, who was waiting nearby; James drove. [10-21-S001]
- **Investigative failures:** A decade without an arrest. Sources reviewed do not identify a specific police error.

## Halloween connection

**Classification: B** (crime occurred during documented Halloween activity).

- Shauna was abducted on **October 27, 1992** while walking home from a **Girl Scout Halloween party**. [10-21-S006, 10-21-S007]
- Body found October 30. **Not A or C.**
- **Aftermath (verified):** From 1993 to 2007, Oil City held trick-or-treating in daylight; council voted each year. In September 2008, council restored evening hours. [10-21-S003, 10-21-S009]
- **Not verified:** that Halloween was "cancelled" or "banned". Wikipedia's "prohibited night-time trick-or-treating for 15 years" and AP's "16 years" both describe a daylight-only rule, not a cancellation. [10-21-S003, 10-21-S008]

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** An 11-year-old Girl Scout walks home from a Halloween party in a small Pennsylvania town. Two blocks from her house, a stranger grabs her in front of a witness. [10-21-S007]
- **Central question:** Who took Shauna, and why did it take a decade to know?
- **Investigation turning point:** Evidence kept since 1992 is matched by DNA in 2002; a third man confesses and names the brothers. [10-21-S001, 10-21-S007]
- **Ending:** Life sentences for two brothers; the third man died in prison in 2022. Oil City kept trick-or-treat in daylight for 15 years until a 10-year-old asked for the night back. [10-21-S003, 10-21-S005]
