## D. Thumbnail concepts (10-09 brand standard)

1. **"The scream upstairs"** — adult woman's face in darkness, eyes wide, lit by a hallway sliver; stairs behind (illustrative survivor POV — not a likeness of Lauren Meanza). White: `NAPA HALLOWEEN MURDERS`. Gold: `SHE HEARD THE SCREAM AT 1 A.M.`
2. **"The cigarettes"** — extreme close-up of a cigarette ember below a dark window, a jack-o'-lantern faintly on the sill. Gold: `HE SMOKED OUTSIDE BEFORE HE WENT IN`.
3. **"Best friend's fiancé"** — two women laughing in soft focus behind a man's shadowed profile (illustrative). Gold: `THE KILLER WAS ALMOST FAMILY`.

Do not show knives, blood, or victims in peril.
