# Script facts — 10-18 Gothenburg discotheque fire ("Backabranden")

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.

# Essential facts

- **Night of Thursday Oct 29 – Friday Oct 30, 1998.** A Halloween party for teenagers in a second-floor hall at Herkulesgatan 1, Backaplan, on Hisingen island in Gothenburg, Sweden. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S003]
- The hall belonged to the Macedonian association and had been rented as a private party. [10-18-S003]
- About **400 young people** were inside. The hall was approved for **150**. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S005]
- To make room for dancing, about two tonnes of tables and chairs had been stacked in the emergency stairwell. [10-18-S008]
- The building had **no fire alarm and no sprinklers**. [10-18-S005]
- Shortly before midnight, someone set fire to the chairs in that stairwell. When the DJ opened the door, smoke poured into the hall. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S005]
- **63 young people died**, aged 12 to 20. More than 200 were injured; 74 went to intensive care. [10-18-S001]

# Timeline

- ~23:42: first alarm call; it came from the DJ, who then escaped through a window. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S005]
- Fire crews arrived within minutes but had to push through crowds. [10-18-S005]
- 01:30: fire under control. 02:02: extinguished. [10-18-S001]
- Days after: rumours spread that it was a racist attack. [10-18-S003]
- Dec 1998: a 3-million-kronor reward. About 1,500 people interviewed. [10-18-S003]
- Jan–Feb 2000: four young men arrested. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S004]
- **Jun 8, 2000**: all four convicted of aggravated arson. [10-18-S009]
- Aug 29, 2000: appeal court raises two sentences. Oct 12, 2000: Supreme Court declines the case. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S009]

# Evidence reveal

- The arsonists were young men of Iranian background, aged 17 to 19. One had been refused free entry; the court found they set the fire in revenge. [10-18-S004, 10-18-S006]
- It was **not** a racist attack, despite the early rumours. [10-18-S003]
- Accident investigators found a door-closing device on the stairwell door had been removed. With the door open, the fire reached the hall far faster. [10-18-S002, 10-18-S007]
- All 63 died from carbon-monoxide poisoning. [10-18-S003]

# Ending

- Sentences: 8 years for the man who lit the fire; 7 years each for two others (raised on appeal from 6); 3 years in closed youth care for the youngest. [10-18-S006]
- The party organisers were not prosecuted. [10-18-S003]
- Families founded BOA, which still supports disaster survivors. [10-18-S003]
- A memorial at Backaplan, unveiled in 2008, carries all 63 names and ages in gold. [10-18-S003]

# Important names

- Backaplan / Herkulesgatan 1, Hisingen, Gothenburg — location
- The Macedonian association — owned the hall
- Statens haverikommission — Swedish Accident Investigation Authority
- BOA (Brandoffrens anhöriga) — families' association
- Claes Hake — memorial sculptor
- Perpetrators — **do not name** (one was a minor)

# Never say

- Never say it happened on Halloween night (Oct 31). It was the night of Oct 29–30.
- Never call it an accident. It was arson; the safety failures explain the death toll, not the cause.
- Never say it was a racist or hate attack. That was a false rumour.
- Never say the four were convicted of murder or of killing 63 people. The conviction is aggravated arson.
- Never name the youngest perpetrator. Avoid naming any of them.
- Never use the case to generalise about immigrants or Muslims. Fringe sites do this.
- Never describe the victims piled in the doorway in graphic terms.
- Never say the fire alarm failed. There was no alarm.
