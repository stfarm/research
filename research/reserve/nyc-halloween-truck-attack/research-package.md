## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks:**
1. Halloween afternoon, 2017. Five friends from Argentina are in New York for their 30-year school reunion. (That they were cycling the path is widely reported but not in the sources read — verify before use.) [S003]
2. He told the FBI he picked Halloween on purpose: more people on the street. [S001]
3. He had rented a truck the week before — just to practise the turns. [S001, S002]

**Suspense beats:** practice rental → GWB crossing at 3 p.m. → turn onto the bike path at Houston → a mile of chaos → school bus at Chambers → he jumps out "armed" → an officer who happened to be at the school a block away → the guns are paintball and pellet guns → that night, the Halloween parade marches anyway.

**Reveal:** The weapons he waved were fakes; the truck was the weapon. The city's answer was to hold the parade.
