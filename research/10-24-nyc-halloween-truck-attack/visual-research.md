1. **"He chose Halloween"** — close on a man's eyes in a truck's rear-view mirror (illustrative, not a likeness; no identifiable features), bike path and cyclists reflected. White: `THE HALLOWEEN ATTACK`. Gold: `HE CHOSE HALLOWEEN FOR MORE VICTIMS`.
2. **"Five friends"** — five middle-aged men laughing by the Hudson (illustrative), skyline behind — add bikes only once cycling is verified. Gold: `30-YEAR REUNION. HALLOWEEN AFTERNOON`.
3. **"The parade marched"** — a costumed crowd at night under police lights. Gold: `HOURS LATER, NEW YORK HELD THE PARADE`.

Never depict the attacker as a hero figure or show the impact.
