# 10-13 — Tokyo Keio Line attack (Chōfu, Tokyo, Halloween 2021)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-01.

> ## READ FIRST
> - **No one died.** This is an **attempted murder and arson** case, not a Halloween murder. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S004]
> - **Final outcome:** 23 years' imprisonment, Tokyo District Court, Tachikawa branch, **July 31, 2023**. Neither side appealed; the sentence became **final on August 14, 2023**. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S004]
> - The costume is context, not the story. Describe it once, plainly (purple suit, green shirt). Do not build the episode around the "Joker" label. [10-13-S005]

---

## Case summary

- On **October 31, 2021**, around **8 p.m.**, a man on a Keio Line limited express bound for Shinjuku stabbed a passenger in the chest. He then moved to another car, spread lighter fluid, and set it alight. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S005]
- The train stopped at **Kokuryō Station** in Chōfu, western Tokyo, after the alarm was raised. Passengers escaped, many through the windows. [10-13-S005, 10-13-S006, 10-13-S008]
- The Tokyo Fire Department counted **17 injured**, three seriously. Not all injuries came from the knife: others came from smoke and the escape. **No one died.** [10-13-S006, 10-13-S007]
- **Kyota Hattori**, 24, was arrested at the scene. He told police he wanted to kill people so he would be sentenced to death. [10-13-S005, 10-13-S007]
- He admitted the stabbing and the fire at trial but denied that he intended to kill anyone with the fire. The court disagreed for most passengers and sentenced him to **23 years**. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S003, 10-13-S010]

## Victim profile

Japanese courts and media did not publish the injured passengers' names. Do not name them.

- **Man stabbed in the chest**, 72 (AP / Japan Times; early Reuters reports said "in his 60s"). He was sitting near the attacker. He needed about three months of treatment. At trial he said he had lasting numbness in his hands, trouble walking, and had to give up work. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S002, 10-13-S004, 10-13-S009]
- **A 21-year-old woman** hyperventilated during the escape. At trial she said she could no longer ride trains, go to school or work, or attend job interviews. [10-13-S004]
- **Other passengers**: about a dozen people in the car where the fire was set; most fire-related injuries were not serious. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S003]
- A victim's courtroom words, as reported: "If you can't take responsibility, don't do it." (「責任を取れないならやるな」) [10-13-S004, 10-13-S013]

## Investigation

- **Discovery / arrest:** There was no manhunt. He was detained at the scene. [10-13-S005]
- **Statements:** He told investigators he wanted to kill people and receive the death penalty. Japanese media also reported (police-sourced) that he said he learned from the August 2021 Odakyu attack and bought the knife online. [10-13-S005, 10-13-S006, 10-13-S007]
- **Motive presented at trial:** He described distress at work and shock that his former girlfriend married someone else about six months after they broke up. He said he wanted to be executed and end his life. [10-13-S001]
- **Premeditation:** Prosecutors argued he picked a limited express with fewer stops so passengers had less chance to escape. [10-13-S001]
- **Key legal issue:** whether setting the fire was *attempted murder* of the passengers in that car. The court found it was for **10** of the 12 passengers named, because they were in concrete danger. It rejected 2 for insufficient proof of their position. [10-13-S003]
- **Investigative failures:** None identified. The policy debate was about train security and evacuation (doors were not opened; passengers climbed out windows). [10-13-S006, 10-13-S012]

## Halloween connection

**Classification: A** (crime occurred October 31).

- The attack happened on Halloween night, October 31, 2021, and Hattori wore a costume resembling the Joker. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S005]
- **Not B:** there is no documented Halloween event he was part of. Passengers were ordinary commuters. Do not imply he targeted Halloween crowds.
- **Not a murder:** no one died.

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** Halloween night 2021, a crowded express into central Tokyo. A passenger is stabbed, a car is set on fire, and commuters climb out through the windows at Kokuryō Station. Seventeen people hurt, no one killed. [10-13-S005, 10-13-S006]
- **Central question:** How close did this come to mass death, and what did the court decide he intended?
- **Investigation turning point:** Not a whodunit. The legal turning point is the court's finding that lighting the fire was attempted murder of the passengers around him. [10-13-S003]
- **Ending:** 23 years, final in August 2023. The stabbed man testified that he could no longer work. [10-13-S004]
