## D. Thumbnail concepts (dedicated covers — follow `docs/publishing/promotional-thumbnail-standard.md`, 10-09 reference)

Minor victim rule applies: depict Lisa respectfully, never in peril close-up, never sexualised.

1. **"The neighbour's porch light"** — foreground: a man's silhouette in a lit doorway (illustrative, not a portrait of Turner), small costumed child seen from behind at the bottom of the steps, felt hat. White: `LISA ANN FRENCH`. Gold: `SHE WENT TRICK-OR-TREATING ALONE`. (Fact: S006.)
2. **"Mother's vigil"** — an adult woman (illustrative of a 1973 mother, not a likeness of Maryann) at a window, porch light, clock near 7. Gold: `HOME BY 7. SHE NEVER CAME BACK`. (Curfew: S002 — verify before use.)
3. **"Still in court in 2027"** — elderly man's face half in shadow behind wire glass (illustrative). White: `THE HALLOWEEN KILLER`. Gold: `53 YEARS LATER, HE WANTS OUT`. (S003; "53 years" = 1973→2026.)

Do not show a body, the bags, or a child's face in fear. Do not present an illustrative face as Lisa or Turner.
