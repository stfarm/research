# Demand research — 10-13 Tokyo Keio Line attack

Audience-interest signals only. Not fact sources.
View counts were scraped from YouTube search results on **2026-10-01**. Dates are YouTube relative ages as of that day. Views are not combined.

## Summary

- **Strong news-clip demand, moderate long-form demand.** Wire clips from 2021 reached 400K–2.1M views; one English long-form explainer has 627K.
- English packaging leans hard on "Joker" ("Japan's Real Life Joker"). That is the angle we are told to avoid, so an accurate, non-sensational version has little direct competition.
- Japanese-language coverage is large (600K–1.9M on news and commentary clips), so the case is well known in Japan.
- Few English videos cover the 2023 verdict or the court's intent finding. That is a differentiator.
