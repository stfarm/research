## Case summary

- Late in the evening of **October 31, 1979**, **Shirley Lynette Ledford**, 16, was hitchhiking home in the northeast San Fernando Valley area of Los Angeles when two men in a van offered her a ride. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S002]
- The men were **Lawrence Bittaker** and **Roy Norris**, recently paroled ex-prisoners who had met in prison. Since June 1979 they had abducted and murdered four other teenage girls. [10-22-S001]
- They killed Shirley that night and left her body in a bed of ivy in front of a house in **Sunland**. An early-morning jogger found her on **November 1, 1979**. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S004]
- In November 1979, Norris told a former prison acquaintance about the killings; that man went to police. A surviving victim of an earlier assault identified the pair from photographs. Both were arrested on **November 20, 1979**. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S004]
- Norris pleaded guilty to five murders and testified. Bittaker was convicted in February 1981 and sentenced to death. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S004]

## Victim profile

- **Shirley Lynette Ledford**, 16 (CDCR/AP and most sources; the 1989 court opinion states 18). [10-22-S001, 10-22-S002, 10-22-S003]
- She was on her way home from her job (court record); several secondary sources say from a Halloween party. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S004, 10-22-S005]
- She was the **only one of the five victims** found soon after her death; the others' remains were either recovered later with Norris's help or never found. [10-22-S001]
- Her mother identified her voice for investigators (Wikipedia). [10-22-S004]
- **Research gap:** no verified biographical material (school, family members' names, interests) was found. Do not invent. Contact family only through appropriate channels; they may not wish to be involved.

**Other victims (named in court and CDCR releases):** Lucinda (Cindy) Schaefer, 16 (June 24, 1979); Andrea Hall, 18 (July 8, 1979 per UPI; court gives July 4 for the abduction); Jacqueline Gilliam, 15, and Leah Lamp, 13 (September 2, 1979). [10-22-S001, 10-22-S002]

## Investigation

- **Discovery:** Shirley's body was left in a residential front yard, not in the mountains like earlier victims. A jogger found her the next morning. [10-22-S001]
- **Break:** Norris boasted to Joseph Jackson, a former prison acquaintance. Jackson, through his lawyer, went to police. [10-22-S004, 10-22-S005]
- **Identification:** A woman who had survived an earlier assault by the pair picked Bittaker out of a photo lineup; that supported the arrest warrant. [10-22-S001]
- **Confession and recovery:** Norris confessed and, under his plea deal, helped investigators find physical evidence and the remains of Jacqueline Gilliam and Leah Lamp. [10-22-S001]
- **Physical evidence:** an audio recording found in Bittaker's van; Shirley's bracelet in Norris's apartment; photographs of victims; the van. [10-22-S001]
- **Witnesses:** motel residents and jail inmates testified about photographs and statements Bittaker made. [10-22-S001]
- **Failures:** Lucinda Schaefer and Andrea Hall were never found. [10-22-S001]

## Halloween connection

**Classification: A** (crime occurred October 31), with **B reported but not confirmed**.

- **A:** Shirley was picked up and killed late on **October 31, 1979**. [10-22-S001]
- **B (unconfirmed):** Secondary sources (Wikipedia, A&E) say she was coming home from a Halloween party. The California Supreme Court says she was "hitchhiking home from her job". Use the court's wording, or say "on Halloween night, on her way home". [10-22-S001, 10-22-S004, 10-22-S005]
- **Not C:** her body was found November 1.

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** On Halloween 1979, a 16-year-old accepts a ride home. The next morning she is found in a front garden in Sunland, the only one of five victims that year found so soon. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S002]
- **Central question:** Who had been taking teenage girls off Southern California roads since June?
- **Investigation turning point:** One of the killers couldn't stop talking. His old prison acquaintance went to police, and a woman who had survived the pair picked them out of a photo lineup. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S004]
- **Ending:** Norris pleads guilty and testifies; Bittaker is sentenced to death. Bittaker dies on death row in 2019 without being executed; Norris dies in prison months later. Two of the five girls were never found. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S002, 10-22-S003]
