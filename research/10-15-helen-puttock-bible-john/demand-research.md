# Demand research — 10-15 Helen Puttock / "Bible John"

Audience-interest signals only. Not fact sources.
View counts were scraped from YouTube search results on **2026-10-01**. Dates are YouTube relative ages as of that day. Views are not combined.

## Summary

- **Strong evergreen demand** for "Bible John" (846K Casual Criminalist; 220K Biographics). Searching "Bible John" alone is polluted by Gospel-of-John audio; titles must say "Glasgow" or "serial killer".
- **Helen Puttock as a search term is weak** (most results under 2K). She is almost always a supporting character in the "Bible John" story. Victim-led framing is a gap.
- Many recent videos push suspect theories ("New Suspect Revealed", "The Suspect Who Fits Every Detail"). An accurate "still unresolved" treatment is a differentiator but competes against more sensational packaging.
