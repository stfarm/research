# 10-07 — Debra Jackson / "Orange Socks"
## Visual research

**Observation date: 30 September 2026.**

**Method and limitation.** No image was downloaded or reproduced. Direct page
retrieval was blocked by the session's network policy, so entries were
identified through search — mostly from the source's own description of what it
showed. **No image was visually inspected.**

**Rights posture:** nothing here is cleared. This case has the **best rights
profile of the three** in this batch, because much of the key imagery originates
with a Texas county sheriff's office, a non-profit, or a public moving-image
archive.

> ### HARD RULE FOR THIS CASE
> **Do not use, generate or commission any AI "reconstruction" of Debra
> Jackson's face.** The 2016 forensic sketch by Natalie Murry is a historical
> artefact and is the thing that actually identified her — her sister recognised
> *that drawing* on television. An AI face would be both factually false and a
> direct insult to the mechanism that named her. Use the real sketches.

- **Entries catalogued: 26** (target 15–25 — slightly over; every entry ties to
  a verified timeline beat).
- **Government/official-origin candidates: 2 sets** (V01, V02 — the WCSO
  forensic sketches). Strongest reusable prospects if requested from the agency.
- **Public archive, permission-realistic: 1 major asset** (V22 — Texas Archive
  of the Moving Image, 1984 KPRC-TV footage). The best archival asset across all
  three cases in this batch.
- **Non-profit, permission-realistic: 1** (V04 — DNA Doe Project).
- **Open-data / open-licence builds: 4** (V13, V14, V15, V17).
- **The one privacy-free scene location in the batch:** V11, the I-35 culvert on
  public highway right-of-way.
- **Nothing was downloaded. Nothing here is cleared.**

### Gaps worth chasing on an unrestricted connection
1. **Williamson County Sheriff's Office** — request the 2016 sketch (and the
   earlier ones) as official releases, and ask whether any family photograph has
   been cleared for media use. Cleanest rights route in this case.
2. **Texas Archive of the Moving Image** — licensing terms for the 1984 KPRC-TV
   footage (V22), and a search for further Orange Socks / Lucas material in
   Texas broadcast collections.
3. **DNA Doe Project** — permission for their case-page imagery, and their own
   account of the genealogy work.
4. **The originating newspaper for V08**, the archival unmarked-graves
   photograph — the most evocative single image located and currently
   rights-unclear.
5. **Williamson County 1979 case file / autopsy report** — would settle the
   discovery time, who found her, and the mile marker, all of which currently
   rest on a secondary blog.
6. **Abilene Reporter-News archives** — likely the only source of pre-1979
   photographs and local family context.
