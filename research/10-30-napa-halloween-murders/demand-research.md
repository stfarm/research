## Interpretation

- **Strongest Halloween-specific long-form demand in the remaining pool.** 48 Hours' upload drew 1.76M in under 12 months — recent, not just evergreen (big channel, so not transferable 1:1).
- Mid-size creators reliably get 70K–860K with "Halloween" in the title; repeated October publication dates show seasonal pull.
- Short-form evidence is thin (≤9K).

## Why it earned 10-30

Second-strongest combination after Halbach: genuine Halloween-night timing, a concrete forensic reveal (cigarette brand → suicide notes → surrender), a close-circle killer, and the best recent demand number in the set.

## Title / curiosity angles (accurate)

- "They Handed Out Halloween Candy. At 1 a.m., Someone Climbed In."
- "The Cigarette Butts Outside Their Window"
- "Her Best Friend's Fiancé"

Avoid: "October 31 murders", "Halloween party massacre".

## Evidence weaknesses

- Largest numbers come from big channels (48 Hours 2.55M, Unseen 1.42M).
- Shorts sample tiny.
