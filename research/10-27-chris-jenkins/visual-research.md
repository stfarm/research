## D. Thumbnail concepts (10-09 brand standard)

1. **"No pockets"** — young man in a plain tan suede costume (illustrative, not a likeness; avoid stereotyped headdress imagery) walking alone onto a lit bridge on a cold night, breath visible. White: `CHRIS JENKINS`. Gold: `HE LEFT HALLOWEEN NIGHT WITH NO COAT`.
2. **"Police apologised"** — close on a police chief's lectern microphones with a missing-poster photo blurred behind. Gold: `"WE DID MAKE A MISTAKE"`. (Quote: JENK-S003.)
3. **"Found in costume"** — frozen river under a steel bridge at dawn, a single fringed suede strip caught on the ice (symbolic). Gold: `FOUND MONTHS LATER STILL IN COSTUME`.

No serial-killer or "smiley face" iconography.
