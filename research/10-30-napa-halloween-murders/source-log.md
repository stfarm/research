Access date for every source: **2026-10-10**. "Fetched" = page text read. "Snippet" = search excerpt only.

- No court opinion exists (guilty plea; appeal waived). The plea and sentencing record is Napa County Superior Court; it was not available online. Contemporaneous Napa Valley Register reporting is the closest primary-adjacent source.
- Napa Valley Register intermittently returned HTTP 429; one article was read in full (S002), others are snippets.
