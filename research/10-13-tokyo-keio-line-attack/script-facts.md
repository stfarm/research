# Script facts — 10-13 Tokyo Keio Line attack

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.

# Essential facts

- **October 31, 2021, around 8 p.m.**: a Keio Line limited express bound for Shinjuku, western Tokyo. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S005]
- A 24-year-old man, **Kyota Hattori**, stabbed a 72-year-old passenger in the chest. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S009]
- He moved to another car, spread lighter fluid from a plastic bottle, and set it on fire. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S006]
- The train made an emergency stop at **Kokuryō Station** in Chōfu. Passengers escaped, many through the windows. [10-13-S005, 10-13-S006]
- **17 people were injured**, 3 seriously (Tokyo Fire Department). **No one died.** [10-13-S006]
- He was arrested at the scene. He told police he wanted to kill people so he would be sentenced to death. [10-13-S005]
- He wore a purple suit and green shirt; media compared it to the Joker. [10-13-S005]

# Timeline

- Aug 2021: a separate knife attack on an Odakyu Line train. Hattori reportedly told police he learned from it. [10-13-S002, 10-13-S007]
- Oct 31, 2021: the attack; arrest at the scene. [10-13-S005]
- Nov 1, 2021: the transport ministry asks rail operators to step up onboard security. [10-13-S012]
- Jun 26, 2023: trial opens at the Tokyo District Court's Tachikawa branch. He admits the stabbing and the fire but denies intending to kill with the fire. [10-13-S010]
- Jul 31, 2023: sentenced to **23 years**. Prosecutors had asked for 25. [10-13-S001]
- Aug 14, 2023: no appeal from either side. The sentence is final. [10-13-S004]

# Evidence reveal

- Identity was never in doubt; he was caught on the train. [10-13-S005]
- The trial turned on intent. The court ruled that setting the fire was **attempted murder** of 10 passengers who were in real danger, and threw out 2 counts. [10-13-S003]
- Judge (as reported): a slight difference in timing could have caused many deaths. [10-13-S003]
- Prosecutors said he chose a limited express with fewer stops so passengers could not easily escape. [10-13-S001]
- His stated motive: trouble at work, and shock that his ex-girlfriend married someone else; he said he wanted to be executed. [10-13-S001]

# Ending

- 23 years in prison, final August 2023. [10-13-S001, 10-13-S004]
- The judge called it "an indiscriminate crime with a selfish motive". [10-13-S001]
- The stabbed man needed about three months of treatment, lost feeling in his hands, and had to give up work. In court he said: "If you can't take responsibility, don't do it." [10-13-S002, 10-13-S004]
- A 21-year-old passenger testified she could no longer ride trains. [10-13-S004]

# Important names

- Kyota Hattori (服部恭太) — offender
- Judge Yu Takeshita — presiding judge, Tachikawa branch
- Kokuryō Station, Chōfu — where the train stopped
- Victims: **unnamed** in Japanese coverage. Keep them unnamed.

# Never say

- Never call it a murder or say anyone died.
- Never say 17 people were **stabbed**. One person was stabbed; the others were hurt by fire, smoke, or the escape.
- Never present the "Joker" as the cause or a motive. He was not found to be acting out a film.
- Never say he attacked Halloween partygoers or a Halloween event. These were ordinary commuters.
- Never present "he wanted to attack women who looked happy" as a court finding. It was a police-sourced media report.
- Never say he can be released in a specific year. No release date is public.
- Never name the injured passengers.
