## Interpretation

- **Evergreen, seasonal.** The two big performers (440K, 301K) are 6–8 years old and from 700K-subscriber channels; both were published in late October. That is evergreen Halloween interest, not current trending.
- **Recent interest (Oct–Nov 2025):** three long-form uploads drew ~20K each on 90K–580K channels — modest.
- **Short-form signal is small but relevant to us:** three 2025 Shorts from channels with **66–111 subscribers** reached 1.2K–3K views — roughly 10–40× their subscriber counts. Suggests the Halloween-night search surface gives small channels some reach. Not a forecast.
- **News hook:** a commitment jury trial is set for **Jan 19, 2027** (S003); coverage will recur.
- No TikTok/Instagram metrics were collected (not publicly accessible from this environment without login).
