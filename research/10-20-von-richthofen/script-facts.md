# Script facts — 10-20 Manfred and Marísia von Richthofen

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.

# Essential facts

- **Manfred Albert von Richthofen**, 49, a German-born engineer and director at São Paulo's state highway company, and **Marísia von Richthofen**, 50, a psychiatrist. [10-20-S002, 10-20-S003]
- They lived in Campo Belo, South Zone of São Paulo, with their children **Suzane**, 19, and **Andreas**, 15. [10-20-S001]
- They opposed Suzane's relationship with **Daniel Cravinhos**. [10-20-S001]
- In the early hours of **October 31, 2002**, they were beaten to death in their bed. [10-20-S001]
- Suzane had let Daniel and his brother **Cristian** into the house; the brothers carried out the killings. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]

# Timeline

- Evening of Oct 30: Suzane and Daniel drop Andreas at an internet café. [10-20-S001]
- Around midnight: the brothers enter the house. [10-20-S002]
- Afterwards: they stage a burglary. Suzane and Daniel go to a motel; Cristian goes elsewhere. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- About 4 a.m., Oct 31: Suzane picks up Andreas; they "find" the break-in and police are called. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- **Nov 8, 2002:** all three confess. [10-20-S002]
- **July 2006:** a jury convicts all three. [10-20-S002, 10-20-S007]

# Evidence reveal

- No forced entry, and the alarm had been switched off. [10-20-S001]
- The "burglars" ransacked one room, left others tidy, and left jewellery and a gun behind. [10-20-S002]
- Money taken included US dollars and euros. Days later, Cristian bought a motorcycle with US dollars, in someone else's name. [10-20-S001, 10-20-S002]
- Under questioning, Suzane confessed, then the brothers. [10-20-S001]

# Ending

- Sentences: Suzane and Daniel, 39 years 6 months; Cristian, 38 years 6 months. [10-20-S007]
- In 2011 a court ruled Suzane unworthy to inherit; her brother Andreas received the estate. [10-20-S002]
- Today all three serve the rest of their sentences in Brazil's **open regime**: living outside prison, working, under curfew and court supervision. Daniel since 2018, Suzane since January 2023, Cristian since March 2025. [10-20-S004]
- Cristian's sentence is projected to end in 2041. [10-20-S005]
