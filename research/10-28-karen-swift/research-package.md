> ## READ FIRST — CRITICAL LEGAL RISK
> - **No one has been convicted.** Her husband **David Swift was acquitted** of first- and second-degree murder (June 6, 2024). The jury hung on voluntary manslaughter; that count was **dismissed as time-barred on March 17, 2025**. [10-28-S002, 10-28-S003]
> - In **March 2026** David Swift sued the county, sheriff and DA in federal court alleging wrongful prosecution. [10-28-S005]
> - Whether the State's appeal of the dismissal (announced March 2025) is pending or resolved is **unknown**. Check before publishing. [10-28-S004]
> - **The story must be told as an unsolved homicide** with an acquitted defendant. Never imply David Swift killed Karen; the prosecution's theory may be presented only as what prosecutors alleged, paired with the verdict.
> - He also faces (faced) an unrelated Alabama stalking charge (2023); status not verified. Do not use it to imply guilt. [10-28-S002]

## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks:**
1. Three weeks after filing for divorce, a mother of four goes to a country-club Halloween party. By morning her car is on the roadside and her phones are smashed. [S001, S002]
2. Six weeks later, a cemetery caretaker finds her under the vines two miles from home. [S002]
3. Eleven years later, her husband was arrested. A jury acquitted him. The case that remains has no killer. [S001, S002]

**Suspense beats:** party → empty car → smashed phones → six-week search → cemetery → a decade cold → 2022 arrest → 2024 trial → acquittal → hung count → dismissed on a clock that ran out → 2026 lawsuit.

**Reveal / question:** Not "who did it" but "how can a case end with no one responsible?" — a jury rejected murder, and the law's own deadline ended manslaughter. [S003]

## Disputed claims, myths, avoid

- **Never** call David Swift the killer, "got away with murder", or "the perfect murder" (creator titles use this — e.g., JustThoughtLounge). Defamation risk.
- **Never** describe the Alabama stalking charge as proof of anything.
- Do not say "solved" or "closed by genealogy" (a creator Short claims genealogy closed the case — false).
- Do not assert the party date as Oct 30 without noting conflict; prefer S001/S002 Oct 29.
- Do not invent a costume, drinks, or who she left with.
- Dustin Swift's laptop testimony: avoid (privacy; contested; excluded at trial per S003).
