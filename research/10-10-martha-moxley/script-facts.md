# Script facts — 10-10 Martha Moxley

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.
**This case is legally unresolved. Every sentence about Michael Skakel must be accurate to the 2018–2020 outcome.**

# Essential facts

- **Martha Moxley**, 15, lived in Belle Haven, Greenwich, Connecticut. [10-10-S001]
- On **October 30, 1975**, the night before Halloween ("mischief night"), she went out with friends around 6:30 p.m. [10-10-S001]
- Around 9 p.m. the group visited the Skakel house across the street. Around **9:30 p.m.** friends last saw her in the Skakel driveway with **Thomas Skakel, 17**. [10-10-S001]
- Between about 9:30 and 10 p.m., neighborhood dogs barked violently and her mother heard voices in the yard. [10-10-S001]
- Her mother called police at **3:48 a.m.** [10-10-S001]
- **October 31, 1975 (Halloween), early afternoon:** her body was found under a pine tree behind her parents' home. She had been beaten with a **golf club** from a set that belonged to the Skakel brothers' late mother. [10-10-S001]

# Timeline

- 1976: police sought an arrest warrant for Thomas Skakel; the prosecutor refused (no probable cause). The tutor, Kenneth Littleton, was also investigated and, per the court, cleared. The case went cold. [10-10-S001]
- Early 1990s: a private firm hired by the Skakel family recorded **both Thomas and Michael Skakel changing their 1975 accounts** of that night. [10-10-S001]
- 1998: Mark Fuhrman's book; a grand jury convened. [10-10-S001, 10-10-S005b]
- **Jan 19, 2000:** Michael Skakel, also 15 in 1975, surrendered. [10-10-S005b]
- **Jun 7, 2002:** a jury **convicted** Michael Skakel of murder. **Aug 29, 2002:** sentenced to 20 years to life. [10-10-S001, 10-10-S016]
- **Oct 23, 2013:** a habeas judge granted a new trial (ineffective counsel). Released on $1.2M bail Nov 21, 2013. [10-10-S005b]
- **Dec 30, 2016:** the Connecticut Supreme Court reinstated the conviction, 4–3. [10-10-S001]
- **May 4, 2018:** on reconsideration, the Connecticut Supreme Court **vacated the conviction**, 4–3, and ordered a new trial. [10-10-S001]
- **Jan 7, 2019:** the U.S. Supreme Court declined Connecticut's appeal. [10-10-S015]
- **Oct 30, 2020:** Chief State's Attorney **Richard J. Colangelo Jr.** announced the state **would not retry** Skakel: "the state cannot prove this case beyond a reasonable doubt." [10-10-S005, 10-10-S006]
- **Dec 24, 2024:** Martha's mother, **Dorthy Moxley**, died at 92. [10-10-S007]

# Evidence reveal

- The weapon came from the Skakel household, but no forensic evidence tied any person to the killing. [10-10-S001]
- The 2018 court called the case "far from overwhelming" and "devoid of any forensic evidence or eyewitness testimony linking [Skakel] to the crime." [10-10-S001]
- The state's key witnesses were two former classmates from a Maine rehab school (Elan) who said Skakel had confessed. One of them, Gregory Coleman, died before trial; his earlier testimony was read to the jury. [10-10-S001]
- The 2018 ruling turned on a witness the jury never heard: **Denis Ossorio**, a disinterested witness who testified at the habeas trial that he saw Michael at his cousin's house, a 15-to-20-minute drive away. [10-10-S001]

# Ending

- No retrial. **Michael Skakel stands unconvicted** and maintains his innocence. [10-10-S005]
- **No one has been convicted of Martha's murder. The case is legally unresolved.** [10-10-S005]
- Martha's brother John said he still believes Skakel did it but accepted the decision. **Attribute it as his opinion.** [10-10-S005]
- Skakel is suing the Town of Greenwich and the lead investigator. Trial was set for November 2026, then delayed (May 2026). [10-10-S008, 10-10-S009]
- In November 2025, NBC News Studios' *Dead Certain* podcast aired Skakel's first public interviews. [10-10-S011]
