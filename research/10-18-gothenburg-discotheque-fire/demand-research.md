# Demand research — 10-18 Gothenburg discotheque fire

Audience-interest signals only. Not fact sources.
View counts were scraped from YouTube search results on **2026-10-01**. Dates are YouTube relative ages as of that day. Views are not combined.

## Summary

- **Strong demand.** Fascinating Horror's short documentary has 650K views; Swedish-language explainers reach 220K–266K.
- English coverage is mostly disaster-history (Fascinating Horror). The criminal investigation and the false racist-attack rumour are less covered in English.
- Swedish audiences engage with survivor and 20/25-year anniversary content.
