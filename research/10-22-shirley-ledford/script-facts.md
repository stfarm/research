# Script facts — 10-22 Shirley Ledford

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`. **No graphic detail. No reference to the recording's content.**

# Essential facts

- **Shirley Lynette Ledford**, 16, of the Los Angeles area. [10-22-S002, 10-22-S003]
- **Late on October 31, 1979**, she was hitchhiking home (the court record says from her job) when two men in a van offered her a ride. [10-22-S001]
- The men were **Lawrence Bittaker** and **Roy Norris**, who had met in prison and been paroled months earlier. [10-22-S001]
- They killed her that night. The next morning, **November 1**, a jogger found her body in the ivy on a front lawn in **Sunland**. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S004]
- She was their fifth victim in five months, and the only one found soon after her death. [10-22-S001]

# Timeline

- June–September 1979: four other teenage girls, Lucinda Schaefer (16), Andrea Hall (18), Jacqueline Gilliam (15) and Leah Lamp (13), disappear after being picked up in the South Bay. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S002]
- Oct 31, 1979: Shirley is picked up. [10-22-S001]
- Nov 1979: Norris tells a former prison acquaintance; that man goes to police. [10-22-S004, 10-22-S005]
- **Nov 20, 1979:** both men arrested. [10-22-S004]
- 1980: Norris pleads guilty to five murders, agrees to testify, and leads investigators to the remains of Jacqueline Gilliam and Leah Lamp. [10-22-S001]
- **Feb 1981:** a jury convicts Bittaker of all five murders. **Mar 24, 1981:** death sentence. [10-22-S002, 10-22-S004]

# Evidence reveal

- A woman who had survived an earlier attack by the pair picked Bittaker out of a photo lineup. [10-22-S001]
- In Bittaker's van, police found evidence tying him to Shirley's death; it was played at trial. **Do not describe it.** [10-22-S001]
- Shirley's bracelet was found in Norris's apartment. [10-22-S001]

# Ending

- **Roy Norris:** 45 years to life. Denied parole in 2019. Died in prison, Feb 24, 2020, aged 72. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S003, 10-22-S006]
- **Lawrence Bittaker:** sentenced to death; the California Supreme Court upheld it in 1989. He was never executed. Died on death row at San Quentin, Dec 13, 2019, aged 79. [10-22-S001, 10-22-S002]
- Lucinda Schaefer and Andrea Hall were never found. [10-22-S001]
- Prosecutor Stephen Kay called the pair "human monsters". [10-22-S002]
