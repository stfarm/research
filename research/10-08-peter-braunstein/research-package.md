# 10-08 — Peter Braunstein (Chelsea, Manhattan, 2005)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01.

> **Survivor privacy rule:** The survivor is a living sexual-assault survivor. She is **not named** anywhere in this package. Some tertiary sources (for example, Wikipedia) print her name. Do not copy it.
>
> **Terminology rule:** Braunstein was convicted of **first-degree sexual abuse**, kidnapping, burglary, and robbery. He was **not convicted of rape**. Some outlets and creator videos use "rape" or "rapist". Do not.

## Case summary

- On Halloween night, October 31, 2005, Peter Braunstein, a 41-year-old former fashion writer, wore a New York City firefighter's uniform. He set off smoke bombs in the building of a former colleague in Chelsea, Manhattan. He then knocked on her door claiming there was a fire or smoke problem. [10-08-S009, 10-08-S010, 10-08-S013]
- Once inside, he used chloroform to incapacitate her. He restrained her and sexually abused her over roughly 13 hours, and videotaped the attack. [10-08-S003, 10-08-S004, 10-08-S010]
- The survivor freed herself and called 911 on the morning of November 1, 2005. [10-08-S010]
- Braunstein fled. The case received heavy tabloid coverage and was featured on *America's Most Wanted*. [10-08-S007, 10-08-S008]
- On December 16, 2005, after about 45 days on the run, a University of Memphis student employee who recognized him from TV alerted campus police. When confronted, Braunstein stabbed himself in the neck and survived. [10-08-S006, 10-08-S007]
- On May 23, 2007, a Manhattan jury convicted him of kidnapping, burglary, sexual abuse, and robbery, and **acquitted him of arson**. [10-08-S003, 10-08-S004]
- On June 18, 2007, Justice Thomas Farber sentenced him to **18 years to life**. [10-08-S001, 10-08-S002, 10-08-S012]
- He was denied parole on August 19, 2025, after waiving his appearance. His next board appearance is August 2027. He is held at Wende Correctional Facility near Buffalo. [10-08-S002]

## Investigation

- **Discovery:** The survivor's 911 call on November 1, 2005 started the case. She described a man in a Fire Department uniform with a knife, gun, and video camera. [10-08-S010]
- **Identification:** After the attack was reported, a woman from the fashion industry contacted police about Braunstein's prior behavior. Her tip helped identify him as the suspect (police account via NBC/MSNBC). [10-08-S009]
- **Physical and digital evidence:** Online orders for firefighter gear, a BB gun, a knife, a replica badge, handcuffs, and smoke-bomb materials, plus a document on his computer laying out a scenario that matched the attack. [10-08-S009, 10-08-S010]
- **Manhunt:** An NYPD fugitive team searched for him. Multiple sightings, several unconfirmed. National exposure on *America's Most Wanted*. [10-08-S007, 10-08-S009]
- **Breakthrough:** Annette Brown, a University of Memphis student employee, recognized him from TV coverage and alerted campus police on December 16, 2005. [10-08-S006, 10-08-S007]

## Halloween connection

**Classification: A — crime occurred on October 31.**

- The attack began on Halloween night, October 31, 2005. It continued into November 1. [10-08-S003, 10-08-S010]
- The firefighter outfit was a **disguise** used to get the survivor to open her door during a staged emergency. No reviewed source states that he chose Halloween so the costume would blend in. **Do not claim that motive.**
- This is not a "Halloween party" case (not class B), and the discovery was not on October 31 (not class C). The 911 call came November 1.
