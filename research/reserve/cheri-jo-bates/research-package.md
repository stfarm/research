## Halloween connection

**Classification: C + Halloween Eve** — killed on the night before Halloween; body found Halloween morning. [10-27-S002, 10-27-S003]

Related, sourced Zodiac-Halloween context (use only as context, not as part of her murder): on **Oct 27, 1970** the Zodiac mailed a **Halloween card** to Chronicle reporter Paul Avery ("Peek a boo you are doomed"). Weeks later (Nov 16, 1970) Avery published the claim tying Bates's case to Zodiac. [10-27-S004, 10-27-S002]

## Investigation

- Physical evidence: paint-spattered Timex watch with torn strap; shoe print; smeared prints on her car; skin and hair under her fingernails (later DNA-profiled as a white male). [10-27-S002]
- 1982 suspect profile (per ABC7, from the report): wore a similar Timex, had dated her shortly before, matched a description of a man seen nearby, boasted of beating polygraphs; DNA compared, no arrest. [10-27-S001]
- Police and friends believe she **knew her killer**. [10-27-S001]

## Legal status (verified 2026-10-10)

**Unsolved homicide.** No arrest, no charges. RPD Homicide Cold Case Unit active; new DNA/fingerprint testing underway (June 2026). Reward $100,000 (private, Ken Welty). [10-27-S001]

## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks:**
1. The night before Halloween 1966, an 18-year-old leaves a note — "Went to RCC Library" — and never comes home. Someone had pulled the wires out of her car. [S002, S001]
2. At dawn on Halloween, a groundskeeper finds her 100 yards from her Volkswagen. [S003, S001]
3. A letter arrives: "Bates had to die. There will be more." For 50 years people asked if it came from the Zodiac. In 2020, DNA on the stamp answered. [S002, S003]

**Suspense beats:** disabled car → the walk away with someone she may have known → screams at 10:15 → dawn discovery → taunting letters → the Zodiac's Halloween card to a reporter → national headlines → decades of theories → stamp DNA unmasks a teenage hoaxer.

**Reveal:** The most famous clue was a prank. The real killer — likely someone she knew — has never been charged.

**Open question:** Will the 2026 DNA testing finally name him?

## Distinguishing details

- Car sabotage (wires pulled) (S001, S002).
- Note to her father (S002).
- Hoax letter writer identified by stamp DNA 50+ years later (S003).
- Zodiac's Halloween card arrived weeks before the Bates–Zodiac headline (S004, S002).

## Disputed claims / myths / avoid

- **Never** call her "the Zodiac's first victim" as fact.
- **Never** say "all the letters were hoaxes" — RPD identified the author of the **1967 handwritten letter**; the 1966 typed "Confession" author is not publicly resolved.
- **Never** name the 1982 suspect, Gary Poste, Ross Sullivan, Arthur Leigh Allen or any person as her killer.
- Do not describe wounds.
- Do not invent Halloween décor or trick-or-treaters on campus.
