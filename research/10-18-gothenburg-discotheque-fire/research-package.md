# 10-18 — Gothenburg discotheque fire ("Backabranden", Hisingen, Sweden, 1998)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-01.

> ## READ FIRST: arson, accident factors, rumours
> - **Arson (established in court):** four young men were convicted of **aggravated arson** (*grov mordbrand*) in 2000. Not murder. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S006, 10-18-S009]
> - **Why so many died (safety failures, not the cause):** about **400 people** in a hall approved for **150**; furniture stacked in the emergency stairwell; **no fire alarm or sprinklers**; a **door closer removed** so the stairwell door could stand open; high, partly barred windows. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S002, 10-18-S005]
> - **Rumours (false):** in the first days, many believed it was a racist attack on immigrant youth. The arsonists were themselves young men of Iranian background; the court found the motive was revenge after being refused free entry. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S004]
> - **Date:** night of **Thursday Oct 29 – Friday Oct 30, 1998**. Not October 31.

## Case summary

- On the night of **October 29–30, 1998**, about **400 young people** aged roughly 12–25 were at a **Halloween party** in a second-floor hall owned by the **Macedonian association** at **Herkulesgatan 1, Backaplan**, on Hisingen island in Gothenburg. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S003, 10-18-S005]
- The hall was approved for 150. It had been rented as a private party. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S003]
- Shortly before midnight, a fire set in a stack of chairs in the emergency stairwell spread smoke and flames into the crowded hall. The stairwell was the second exit; it became impassable. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S005]
- **63 young people died**, most from carbon-monoxide poisoning. More than 200 were injured; 74 went to intensive care. It is one of the deadliest fires in modern Swedish history. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S003]
- After a 15-month investigation, four young men were arrested. All were convicted of aggravated arson in 2000. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S006]

## Victim profile

- **63 dead**, aged **12 to 20**, most 14–18. [10-18-S001]
- Most attendees had immigrant or ethnic-minority backgrounds; Gothenburg's Hisingen and northeastern suburbs were heavily affected. [10-18-S004]
- Relatives founded **BOA (Brandoffrens anhöriga)** in February 1999. It has since supported survivors of other disasters, including the 2004 tsunami. [10-18-S003]
- The **memorial at Backaplan** (sculptor **Claes Hake**, unveiled October 2008) lists all 63 names and ages in gold on blue granite. [10-18-S003]
- **Research gap:** individual victim biographies were not gathered. If any are featured, use family-approved material (BOA) only.

## Investigation

- **Scale:** about 1,500 people interviewed on ~2,000 occasions; cost ~30 million kronor (Swedish Wikipedia, citing official sources). [10-18-S003]
- **Technical investigation:** fire tests (SP) and computer modelling showed the lower door of the emergency stairwell had to be open for the fire to reach the hall so fast; a door-closing device had been removed. [10-18-S002, 10-18-S007]
- **Breakthrough:** arrests came 15 months later after a large reward and extensive interviewing (sources reviewed do not detail the specific tip). [10-18-S003]
- **Motive found:** one of the group was refused free entry; the group set the fire in revenge. Reported accounts say they meant to disrupt the party. [10-18-S004, 10-18-S007]
- **Organisers:** not prosecuted. The prosecutor's 1999 decision noted they had not sought the required permits. [10-18-S003]
- **Failures (systemic):** overcrowding; blocked exit; no alarm; door closer removed; venue not designed for events; dispatcher difficulty hearing the address. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S005, 10-18-S008]

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** A Halloween party for teenagers, 400 people in a room meant for 150, and the second exit piled with chairs. Someone sets those chairs alight. [10-18-S001, 10-18-S005]
- **Central question:** Who set the fire, and why did so many die?
- **Investigation turning point:** After 15 months and a 3-million-kronor reward, four young men are arrested; the motive is not hate but a refused free entry. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S004]
- **Ending:** Arson convictions in 2000; sentences of 3 to 8 years. A memorial with 63 names stands at Backaplan; families founded BOA. [10-18-S003, 10-18-S006]
