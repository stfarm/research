# 10-15 — Helen Puttock / "Bible John" (Glasgow, 1969)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-01.

> ## STATUS — READ FIRST
> - **Unresolved.** No one has ever been charged with Helen Puttock's murder. [10-15-S005, 10-15-S013]
> - **"Bible John" is a nickname for an unidentified man**, the man who shared Helen's taxi. Whether he also killed Patricia Docker (1968) and Jemima MacDonald (1969) is a **long-standing theory**, not an established fact. Police Scotland refers to three *unresolved* murders. [10-15-S013]
> - **No named suspect has been proven to be Bible John.** John Irvine McInnes was formally cleared by the Crown in 1996. Peter Tobin was discounted by police. [10-15-S005, 10-15-S001]

## Case summary

- On **Thursday, October 30, 1969**, **Helen Puttock**, 29, went dancing at the **Barrowland Ballroom** in Glasgow's East End with her sister **Jean ("Jeannie")**. [10-15-S002, 10-15-S011]
- The sisters met two men who both said they were called John. One left to catch a bus. The other shared a taxi west with the sisters. [10-15-S001, 10-15-S002]
- Jeannie was dropped off first in Knightswood. Helen stayed in the taxi with the man. [10-15-S002]
- Helen's body was found the next morning, **Friday, October 31, 1969**, by a man walking his dog behind her tenement on **Earl Street, Scotstoun**. She had been beaten, sexually assaulted, and strangled. [10-15-S002, 10-15-S011]
- Jeannie gave police a detailed description of the man, including that he quoted the Bible. The press named him **"Bible John"**. [10-15-S001, 10-15-S002]
- He has never been identified. The case remains open. [10-15-S005, 10-15-S013]

## Victim profile

- **Helen Puttock**, 29, of Earl Street, Scotstoun, Glasgow. Mother of two young sons, **David** and **Michael**. [10-15-S002, 10-15-S005]
- Husband **George Puttock**, a soldier. He had just come home on leave and did not want her to go out that night. [10-15-S005]
- Her sons were raised in England by George and his second wife, **Mavis**. Michael died in 2020. David lives in Canada. [10-15-S005, 10-15-S006]
- George spent the rest of his life seeking answers. He died in November 2022. Shortly before, he said: "I have to accept that I will probably never know who killed my Helen and the other women." [10-15-S005, 10-15-S006]
- Son David (2022): "I wish that police could look at the murders again and try to get some answers for the families." [10-15-S006]

## Investigation

- **Discovery:** Helen was found by a man walking his dog behind her own tenement on the morning of October 31. [10-15-S002, 10-15-S011]
- **Eyewitness:** Jeannie spent about an hour with the man (dance hall, taxi rank, taxi). Her description: about 25–30, 5'10"–6', reddish or sandy hair, blue-grey eyes, well dressed, with overlapping front teeth. He quoted scripture and spoke disapprovingly of women at dance halls. [10-15-S001]
- **Public appeal:** The inquiry released a photofit and a painted portrait. Wikipedia says this was the first use of photofit in a Scottish murder inquiry. [10-15-S001]
- **Scale:** Wikipedia reports 100+ detectives, ~50,000 statements, and thousands of men questioned. Treat figures as approximate. [10-15-S001]
- **Forensics:** A semen stain on Helen's stockings and hair near the body were preserved. A bite mark on her wrist was recorded. [10-15-S005, 10-15-S008]
- **McInnes (1996):** DNA comparison after exhumation provided no evidence he was the source; the Crown formally cleared him. Jeannie had also said he was not the man. [10-15-S001, 10-15-S005, 10-15-S008]
- **Tobin:** Speculation followed his 2007–2009 murder convictions. Police said there was no evidence linking him; reported records place him in Brighton in late 1969. [10-15-S001]
- **Alleged failure (theory):** The 2022 BBC podcast alleged senior officers protected McInnes because his cousin was a senior detective. This is **an allegation**. Police are assessing it. [10-15-S003, 10-15-S004]
- **Linking the three murders:** All three women had been at the Barrowland (Docker uncertain) and were killed in similar ways. Police at the time linked them publicly; today they are treated as three unresolved murders. Whether one man killed all three is unproven. [10-15-S001, 10-15-S013]

## Halloween connection

**Classification: C** (major discovery on October 31), with A probable.

- Helen's body was found on the morning of **October 31, 1969**. [10-15-S011]
- She left the dance late on October 30 and was killed in the early hours, most likely on October 31. Exact time of death is not established in sources reviewed.
- Her night out was an ordinary Thursday dance, not a Halloween event. **Not B.**

## Story structure

- **Strongest factual hook:** The only time anyone spent an evening with Glasgow's most notorious unidentified suspect, it was Helen's sister. She shared his taxi, heard him quote the Bible, and got out first. [10-15-S001, 10-15-S002]
- **Central question:** Who was the man in the taxi?
- **Investigation turning point:** In 1996 police exhumed their best suspect to test his DNA. It did not match. [10-15-S005, 10-15-S008]
- **Ending:** Still unsolved. The witness died in 2010, Helen's husband in 2022, and police were still assessing new claims in 2023. [10-15-S003, 10-15-S005, 10-15-S007]
