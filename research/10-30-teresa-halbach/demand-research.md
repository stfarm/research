## Interpretation

- **Recognition: the highest in the October slate.** Netflix trailer 4.8M; counter-documentary episode 4.4M (2023).
- **Recent interest is real:** Sept 2026 uploads (76K in ~1 month on a 137K channel) followed the Sept 2026 commutation news (10-31-S004). Halloween 2025 was the 20th anniversary (WBAY coverage).
- **Halloween angle is under-used:** searches pairing "Teresa Halbach" with "Halloween" return few videos (largest: a German podcast, 27,835 views). That is an open lane, not proof of demand.
- **Shorts:** Daily Wire Short ~15K on a 3.2M channel — weak relative to channel size. Large-channel short-form demand for this case is not established.
- Audience is polarised (guilty vs framed). Engagement may be high; so is comment-section risk.

## Why it earned 10-30 (moved from 10-31 on 2026-10-11)

Highest recognition in the slate, a court-documented Halloween disappearance and a live 2026 news hook. Moved to 10-30 so the finale goes to Napa (10-31): Halbach's huge numbers are largely *Making a Murderer* **franchise** demand rather than Halloween-story demand (Halloween-specific Halbach videos are small — largest 27,835), and its defamation/legal risk is higher than Napa's.
