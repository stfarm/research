# 10-26 — Robin Hoynes (Torrance, California, 1984)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-10.

> ## READ FIRST
> - Killed the **night before Halloween** (Oct 30, 1984); found **Halloween morning**. Halloween class **C + Eve** — no party/costume element. [10-26-S001, 10-26-S002, 10-26-S003]
> - **Solved in court:** William Charles Marshall convicted of first-degree murder (Oct 2007), life sentence. Whether LWOP is **not confirmed**. Current custody not verified. [10-26-S002, 10-26-S003, 10-26-S004]
> - The conviction rested on **circumstantial** evidence (DDA's own words) — recanted alibi, foam/boot theory, motive, key, knife. The FBI could not chemically confirm the foam match. Do not call it "DNA" or "forensic proof". [10-26-S001, 10-26-S004]
> - Lower-recognition case (one 551K creator video; little else). Included for strong story mechanics and genuine Halloween timing.

## Halloween connection

**Classification: C + Halloween Eve.** Killed while closing on Oct 30; discovered by the co-worker whose shift she had covered on Halloween morning. [10-26-S001, 10-26-S003]

- Sourced Halloween aftermath: at the 2007 sentencing her sister said the family would hand out Halloween candy at their mother's home for the first time in 23 years. [10-26-S002]
- Not sourced: costumes, decorations, Halloween plans. Do not add.

## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks:**
1. She only worked that night because she swapped shifts as a favour. The co-worker who asked found her body the next morning — Halloween. [S003]
2. The man fired from that restaurant four days earlier still had a key. [S001]
3. Police had him in 1984. Then they let him go — and he became a fire captain. [S001]

**Suspense beats:** shift swap → closing alone → Halloween-morning discovery → $600 still in the safe → fired manager with a key → alibi from his girlfriend → DA declines → 20 years → girlfriend recants → a sliver of foam and a worn boot heel → arrest at a firehouse.

**Reveal:** The alibi was a lie; the man trusted to save lives was the killer (per the 2007 verdict).

## Distinguishing details

- Shift swap (S003).
- Key + firing four days earlier (S001).
- Foam from a worn boot heel (S002; contested by FBI chemistry, S004).
- Killer later became a CAL FIRE captain, arrested at his firehouse (S001).
- Family hands out Halloween candy again after 23 years (S002).

## Disputed claims / myths / avoid

- Not DNA. Not "forensic match proved".
- "Found stabbed" is enough; no wound detail.
- Do not name Cheryl Fuller in a way that implies blame (she swapped shifts innocently).
- Hargrove's account is testimony from a recanted alibi witness — attribute it.
- Some creators call Robin "manager"; she was assistant manager.
