# Script facts — 10-11 Taylor Van Diest

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.

# Essential facts

- **Taylor Jade Van Diest**, 18, of Armstrong, B.C. A twin; she and her sister Kirstie graduated from Pleasant Valley Secondary in June 2011. [10-11-S014]
- **October 31, 2011:** dressed as a zombie, she walked to meet friends for Halloween around 6 p.m., taking a shortcut along the railway tracks. [10-11-S002, 10-11-S008, 10-11-S013]
- **6:01 p.m.:** she texted a friend she was "being creeped". It was her last message. [10-11-S008]
- Around **8:30 p.m.**, searchers including family and friends found her badly injured near the tracks. [10-11-S002, 10-11-S003]
- She died **November 1, 2011**, at Kelowna General Hospital. [10-11-S008]
- She did not know her attacker. [10-11-S008]

# Timeline

- Nov 2011: police released photos of her zombie costume. About 1,250 tips followed. [10-11-S001, 10-11-S012]
- **Apr 4, 2012:** Matthew Foerster, 26, formerly of Cherryville, B.C., arrested in Ontario. His father was arrested too. [10-11-S001]
- **Apr 5, 2014:** a jury convicted Foerster of first-degree murder (life, 25 years without parole). [10-11-S003, 10-11-S011]
- May 2014: his father pleaded guilty as an accessory after the fact; 3 years less time served. [10-11-S004]
- Dec 2014: Foerster pleaded guilty to two earlier attacks on women (2004 Cherryville, 2005 Kelowna). [10-11-S005]
- **Mar 7, 2017:** the B.C. Court of Appeal ordered a new trial over errors in the judge's jury instructions. [10-11-S006]
- **Mar 12, 2018:** Foerster pleaded guilty to **second-degree murder**. No retrial. [10-11-S007]
- **Jun 4, 2018:** life sentence, no parole eligibility for **17 years**. [10-11-S008]

# Evidence reveal

- **DNA** from Taylor's case matched DNA from an unsolved 2005 sexual assault in Kelowna. That match led police to Foerster. [10-11-S001, 10-11-S003]
- He had been working in Ontario using **fake ID his father paid $500 for**. His father also lied to police and coached him on avoiding detection. [10-11-S004]
- Per the facts reported at sentencing, she was struck repeatedly with a heavy flashlight and strangled with a shoelace. [10-11-S008, 10-11-S009]

# Ending

- **Final conviction: second-degree murder** (by guilty plea, 2018). Sentence: life; parole ineligibility 17 years. [10-11-S007, 10-11-S008]
- **Parole eligibility: April 4, 2029.** He may apply under the "faint hope" clause from **April 4, 2027** (reported). [10-11-S008]
- Foerster in court: "I'm so sorry that I have taken your loved one." [10-11-S008]
- Her mother: "There will always remain an empty chair." [10-11-S008]
- A memorial trail in Armstrong carries her name. Volunteers maintained it in 2026. [10-11-S014, 10-11-S016]

# Never say

- Never say his **final** conviction is first-degree murder. It is **second-degree** (2018 plea).
- Never say "25 years without parole" as the current sentence. It is **17 years**.
- Never say the appeal court found him innocent or "freed" him. It ordered a new trial, and he stayed in custody and pleaded guilty.
- Never say Taylor **died** on Halloween. She was attacked Oct 31 and **died Nov 1**.
- Never say DNA came "from under her fingernails". Unverified. Say "DNA evidence".
- Never say Foerster "will be released in 2027" or "2029". Those are only eligibility dates; release is not automatic.
- Never present the sexual-assault-intent finding as final. It came from the 2014 verdict, which was set aside.
