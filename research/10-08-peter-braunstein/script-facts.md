# Script facts — 10-08 Peter Braunstein

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.

# Essential facts

- Halloween night, **October 31, 2005**, Chelsea, Manhattan. [10-08-S003, 10-08-S010]
- Peter Braunstein, **41**, a former fashion writer at Fairchild Publications (*Women's Wear Daily* / *W*). [10-08-S003, 10-08-S007]
- He wore a **New York City firefighter's uniform**, set off **smoke bombs** in the building, and knocked on a former colleague's door saying there was a fire. [10-08-S004, 10-08-S010]
- Inside, he used **chloroform**, restrained her, and **sexually abused her for about 13 hours**. He videotaped it. [10-08-S003, 10-08-S004]
- He had bought firefighter gear, a BB gun, a knife, a replica police badge, handcuffs, and smoke-bomb materials online weeks earlier (trial evidence). [10-08-S010]
- The survivor freed herself and **called 911 on the morning of November 1**. [10-08-S010]

# Timeline

- Oct 31, 2005: attack begins. [10-08-S003]
- Nov 1, 2005: 911 call. [10-08-S010]
- Nov–Dec 2005: manhunt. Featured on *America's Most Wanted*. [10-08-S007, 10-08-S008]
- **Dec 16, 2005**: recognized at the University of Memphis by student employee Annette Brown, who had seen him on TV. He stabbed himself in the neck when confronted and survived. About **45 days** on the run. [10-08-S006, 10-08-S007]
- **May 23, 2007**: convicted of kidnapping, burglary, first-degree sexual abuse, and robbery. **Acquitted of arson.** Jury took under four hours. [10-08-S003, 10-08-S004]
- **June 18, 2007**: sentenced to **18 years to life** by Justice Thomas Farber. [10-08-S001, 10-08-S002]
- **Aug 19, 2025**: first parole hearing. He waived his appearance and was **denied**. [10-08-S002]
- **Aug 2027**: next parole board appearance. [10-08-S002]

# Evidence reveal

- Planning evidence was central at trial: online orders for the uniform, smoke-bomb materials, a BB gun, and a fake badge, bought weeks ahead. [10-08-S010]
- The survivor's 911 call: "a Fire Department uniform", "a smoke bomb or something", "a knife, a gun and a video camera". [10-08-S010]
- Defense said he was too mentally ill to form intent and showed brain scans. The jury rejected that in under four hours. [10-08-S003, 10-08-S004]

# Ending

- Serving 18 years to life at Wende Correctional Facility, near Buffalo, NY. [10-08-S002]
- At sentencing he said: "In fact, I did plan the crime. The madness resides in the fact that I had no motive or intent." [10-08-S005]
- In 2025, the Manhattan DA told the parole board he had said he "regret[ted] the choice of victim, but not the crime itself." [10-08-S002]
- He skipped his first parole hearing and was denied. Next chance: **August 2027**. [10-08-S002]
