# 10-24 — Cindy Song (State College, Pennsylvania, Halloween 2001)

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-11. Replaces the NYC Halloween truck attack in this slot (that package is preserved at [`../reserve/nyc-halloween-truck-attack/`](../reserve/nyc-halloween-truck-attack/)).

> ## READ FIRST
> - **This is an unresolved disappearance.** No body, no arrest, no charge. Police have never established that a crime occurred; in 2002 the lead detective said "There's no evidence of foul play or that a crime's been committed," while also saying her leaving "was not something she planned." Never call it a kidnapping or murder. [SONG-S002, SONG-S003]
> - **The Hugo Selenski connection is unproven.** It came from an informant (Paul Weakley) who had researched the case online; no DNA from Selenski's property matched her; police say they have "never been able to prove or disprove" it. Present it only as a lead that went nowhere. [SONG-S003]
> - Halloween fit: she spent **Halloween night in a bunny costume at a club party** and vanished hours later, after a friend dropped her home at ~4 a.m. on **Nov 1**. Class **B** (Halloween activity) — not an Oct 31 event. [SONG-S001, SONG-S002]
> - Her family is living and in South Korea; her mother's current status is unknown (SONG-S003). Treat with care.

## Halloween connection

**Classification: B.** Halloween night (Wednesday, Oct 31, 2001) she went to a **Halloween party at Players Nite Club**, 112 W. College Ave., dressed as a "Playboy bunny" — white skirt with a cotton tail, pink sleeveless bunny shirt, red hooded coat, brown suede boots, bunny ears and false eyelashes. [SONG-S002]

- She was last seen in the early hours of Nov 1. Do not say "she vanished on Halloween"; say "after Halloween night" or "hours after her Halloween night out".
- The bunny costume was never found; the false eyelashes were found in the apartment. [SONG-S002, SONG-S004]

## Investigative / legal status

- **Searched 2026-10-11. Latest corroborated status: Oct 29, 2025** (Onward State) — case open with Ferguson Township PD; no arrests; no remains found. No 2026 developments found.
- No suspect has ever been charged. The Selenski lead is "never … proven or disproven" (Mayer, 2022). [SONG-S003]
- Police ruled voluntary departure unlikely (registered for classes, ordered a computer, bought concert tickets). [SONG-S002, SONG-S003]

## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks (factual):**
1. Halloween 2001. A Penn State senior in a bunny costume dances at a downtown club, gets a ride home at 4 a.m., and walks up the stairs to her apartment. Nobody has seen her since. [SONG-S001, SONG-S003]
2. Inside, police found her backpack, her phone and the false eyelashes she'd worn that night. Her keys, purse and the bunny costume were gone. [SONG-S002, SONG-S004]
3. She had concert tickets, a new computer on order and classes booked for spring. [SONG-S002]

**Suspense beats:** club → late-night stops → Uni-Mart → 4 a.m. drop-off → silent phone → missed shift → apartment search (what's there, what isn't) → a taxi at the same complex at 4 a.m. → a Philadelphia "abduction" sighting that wasn't her → an informant's story that led to a property full of remains — none of them hers.

**Reveal / question:** Every piece of evidence says she made it home. Nothing explains how she left. [SONG-S005]

## Distinguishing details

- Eyelashes removed but costume gone (S002, S005).
- 4 a.m. taxi passenger police wanted to trace (S001).
- Psychic and paranormal society consulted in 2002 (S003) — colourful but low-value; use sparingly.

## Disputed claims / myths / avoid

- **Never** say she was kidnapped, murdered, held in a vault, or killed by Selenski.
- **Never** name Stacey/Stacy Paik as a suspect or imply fault (she is a witness who drove her home).
- **Never** name the 2002 boyfriend or any acquaintance as a suspect.
- Avoid the Charley Project's detail that "Selenski told police" — conflicts with 2022 reporting (informant Weakley).
- Avoid "Playboy bunny" framing in titles/thumbnails (sexualisation risk); "bunny costume" is enough.
- Avoid diary/drug-use details (S006 says the drug theory was discarded).
- The family's apartment cleaning: sensitive; if used, attribute and don't blame.
