> ## READ FIRST
> - **Official status:** Minneapolis police reclassified the death as a **homicide** in Nov 2006 and said he was **thrown from a bridge**, based on an eyewitness who was also a possible suspect. In 2008 MPD said there was "not sufficient, demonstrable evidence to support a criminal prosecution." **No one has been charged.** Attribute "thrown from a bridge" to police; do not present it as a proven fact. [JENK-S003, JENK-S005]
> - **"Smiley Face Killers" is a theory, not a finding.** MPD: "we can neither confirm nor endorse" it; police said in 2006 that his death was not linked to other drownings. Do not suggest a serial killer. [JENK-S005, JENK-S003]
> - **Never name the incarcerated witness/person of interest** or anyone named in secondary sources.
> - Halloween fit: **A + B** — out on Halloween night itself in costume; found months later still in the costume. [JENK-S001, JENK-S008]

## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks:**
1. Halloween 2002. A college lacrosse captain in a suede costume with no pockets hands his wallet and phone to his girlfriend at a downtown bar. He walks out alone, without a coat, into a cold Minneapolis night. [JENK-S001]
2. Four months later he's pulled from the Mississippi — still in costume. Police call it an accident. [JENK-S003, JENK-S004]
3. Four years later the police chief apologises: "We did make a mistake." His death is now a homicide. [JENK-S003]

**Suspense beats:** costume with no pockets → leaves alone → last seen near the Hennepin Avenue Bridge → winter search of the river → body in February → "accident" → parents' private investigation → a detective follows a rumor → an eyewitness in prison → "thrown from a bridge" → no charges, ever.

**Reveal / question:** Police say someone threw him off a bridge, and they believe they know who saw it — but in 2008 they said they could not prove it. [JENK-S003, JENK-S005]
