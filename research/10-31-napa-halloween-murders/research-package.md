# 10-31 — The Napa Halloween murders: Adriane Insogna & Leslie Mazzara (Napa, California, 2004)

> **Slot change (2026-10-11):** moved from 10-30 to **10-31** (Halloween finale); folder renamed from `10-30-napa-halloween-murders/`. Source IDs keep their original `10-30-Sxxx` form so existing citations stay valid.

Research package. Not a script. Researched 2026-10-10.

> ## READ FIRST
> - The attack happened **just after 1 a.m. on Nov 1, 2004**, hours after the roommates handed out Halloween candy. It is "Halloween night" in common usage, but the calendar date is **Nov 1**. Say "on Halloween night" only as "the night of Halloween", never "on October 31". [10-30-S001, 10-30-S002]
> - Solved. **Eric Copple** pleaded guilty to two counts of first-degree murder; **life without parole**; appeal waived. [10-30-S001, 10-30-S002]
> - **Lauren Meanza** survived. She is a living person; she has given interviews (ABC, per S004). Use her name only as published; no speculation about her.
> - Lily Prudhomme (Copple's wife) was **never accused** of anything. Do not imply knowledge or involvement.
> - People's Nov 2025 recap has wrong years. Use 2004 (crime) and 2005 (arrest).

## Halloween connection

**Classification: A\* (night of Oct 31 – Nov 1)** — the attack came in the first hours after Halloween, after the roommates had spent the evening **handing out Halloween candy**. [10-30-S001]

- Additional sourced context: the killer later said he had been drinking heavily on Halloween night and Lily refused to spend the night with him at their apartment (DA's account). [10-30-S001]
- Unverified/avoid: costumes, a Halloween party at the house. No source puts the victims at a party.
- Eerie but sourced: Copple and Lily had originally planned to marry in Hawaii on **November 1, 2004** — the day of the murders — but had put those plans on hold beforehand. Lily: "if we had gone through with that wedding … Adriane and Lauren would have been in Hawaii with us that week." [10-30-S001]

## Story engine (angles, not script)

**Opening hooks:**
1. Three roommates hand out Halloween candy and go to bed. Just after 1 a.m., one of them hears a scream upstairs. [S001]
2. The killer smoked outside their window before he went in — and left the butts behind. [S001]
3. The man who did it was no stranger: he was her best friend's longtime boyfriend — and within months of the murders, her husband. Adriane's mother read scripture at their wedding. [S001]

**Suspense beats:** scream → footsteps on the stairs → the intruder flees past the survivor through a window → a year of DNA swabs from 200+ men → police reveal the cigarette brand → suicide notes → family drives him to police.

**Reveal:** The killer was close to the victims: the best friend's partner, who married her while the case was unsolved. He said he did it with "his eyes closed"; the DA said he "knew exactly what he did". [S001]

**Unresolved question:** Why Leslie, whom he barely knew? No motive has ever been given for her. [S001, S002]

## Distinguishing details

- Camel Turkish Gold cigarette butts smoked to the filter (S001).
- He and Lily had once planned to marry on **November 1, 2004**, the day of the murders (plans put on hold beforehand) (S001).
- The DA: "It's hard to believe that she laid next to him 11 months and didn't have any suspicions" — the 11 months refers to the time from the murders to his arrest, not to the wedding (S001). There is **no evidence Lily knew**; CBS: "There is no proof that Lily knew."
- He burned his clothes in a fire pit at home (his account, S001).
- Gag clause: he can never tell the story to media or authors (S003).
- Arlene Allen's turtleneck line (S002) — powerful but references wounds; use carefully.
