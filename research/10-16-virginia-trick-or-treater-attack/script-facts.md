# Script facts — 10-16 Virginia Halloween trick-or-treater attack / "East Coast Rapist"

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`. **Survivors are never named.**

# Essential facts

- **Halloween night, October 31, 2009**, Dale City, Prince William County, Virginia. [10-16-S005]
- Three teenage girls, aged 16, 17 and 17, were walking home after a birthday party and trick-or-treating. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S007]
- Near a shopping centre on Dale Boulevard, a masked man asked them for money, then forced them, with what appeared to be a gun, into the woods behind the stores. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S007]
- He assaulted the two older girls. [10-16-S007]
- The 16-year-old secretly texted her mother and friends to call 911. When sirens approached, he ran. [10-16-S005, 10-16-S009]

# Timeline

- 1997–2009: DNA links the same unknown man to attacks on women in Maryland, Virginia, Connecticut and Rhode Island. Police call him the "East Coast Rapist". [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- Oct 31, 2009: the Dale City attack; his last known attack. [10-16-S001]
- Late Feb 2011: digital billboards with sketches go up in seven states, and a website launches. [10-16-S002, 10-16-S003]
- Days later: a tip names **Aaron H. Thomas**, 39, a trucker from New Haven, Connecticut. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- Mar 3, 2011: officers follow him and pick up a cigarette butt he throws away. [10-16-S002]
- **Mar 4, 2011**: the DNA matches. He is arrested at home. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- Nov 2012: he pleads guilty in Virginia. [10-16-S007, 10-16-S008]
- **Mar 1, 2013**: sentenced to life terms for the Halloween attack. [10-16-S008]
- Jun 2015: pleads guilty to three rapes in Maryland; another life sentence. [10-16-S010]

# Evidence reveal

- The scene DNA matched an existing series but no name; his DNA was in no database. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- Investigators had ruled out more than 700 men. [10-16-S002]
- The website drew 44,000 hits in 12 hours. [10-16-S002]
- The cigarette butt matched. [10-16-S001, 10-16-S002]
- On arrest, a Connecticut prosecutor said, Thomas asked: "Why haven't you picked me up sooner?" [10-16-S003]
- A detective testified Thomas said that when he saw the girls, he "had gotten an urge". [10-16-S005]

# Ending

- Convicted on guilty pleas of two rapes and three abductions for Halloween 2009; three life terms (Mar 1, 2013). [10-16-S007, 10-16-S008]
- More life sentences followed for a 2001 Leesburg attack (Virginia) and three Maryland rapes (2015). [10-16-S010, 10-16-S012]
- In court, two survivors said they had forgiven him; one said he should never get out. [10-16-S008]
