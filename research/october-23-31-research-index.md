## Folder contents (each case)

- `research-package.md` — identity, Halloween connection, chronology, legal status, hook angles, suspense beats, reveal, distinguishing details, myths/avoid, pronunciations, production assessment
- `source-log.md` — source IDs, publishers, URLs, dates, access dates, what each supports, quality notes, contradictions
- `visual-research.md` — free images, identified reference images (with direct URLs and identification checks), recreation ideas, three thumbnail concepts per the 10-09 cover standard

## Method notes and limitations

- **YouTube metrics:** exact publish dates, view counts and subscriber counts were read from YouTube's public web endpoints on 2026-10-10. Values marked "search" or "rounded" come from search-result listings only. No TikTok/Instagram metrics (login walls). No search-volume or CTR claims.
- **Blocked sources:** CourtListener, Justia (partly), AP, Press-Enterprise, Fond du Lac Reporter, Chicago Tribune, Court TV — facts from these appear only as labelled snippets. justice.gov was read via headless rendering.
- **Images:** Wikimedia Commons rate-limited this session (HTTP 429). Failed downloads are listed in each `images/manifest.md` with Commons page URLs for manual retrieval. Copyrighted reference images were opened only to identify them and are **not** stored in the repo.
- **Legal status** verified as of 2026-10-10. Re-check 10-23, 10-25, 10-28 and 10-31 in the week of publication.
