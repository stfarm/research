# Script facts — 10-09 Rebekah Gay

Verified facts only. Source IDs refer to `source-log.md`.

# Essential facts

- **Rebekah Jane Gay**, 24, born March 26, 1988, in Midland, Michigan. [10-09-S001, 10-09-S009]
- Mother of a **3-year-old son**. Recently promoted to management at the Goodwill store in Mount Pleasant. Engaged. Her family called her "Bekah-Boo". [10-09-S009]
- Killed at her home in rural Isabella County in the **early morning hours of October 31, 2012** (official prosecutor release). [10-09-S001]
- **John Douglas White**, 55, pastor of Christ Community Fellowship, a 14-member church. He regularly babysat her son and was engaged to her mother (AP reporting). [10-09-S004, 10-09-S005]
- White was a paroled ex-convict. He served nearly **12 years for manslaughter** in a 1995 Kalamazoo County death and was released in 2007. In 1981 he received probation for **choking and stabbing a 17-year-old girl**. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004]

# Timeline

- Oct 31, 2012, early morning: Rebekah killed at home. Her son was in the home. [10-09-S001, 10-09-S006]
- Oct 31, 2012: she didn't arrive at work. Her car was found behind a nearby bar. A search began. [10-09-S010]
- About 20 hours of searching. Her mother called it "excruciating". [10-09-S006]
- **Nov 1, 2012**: according to the sheriff, White confessed after investigators told him her body would deteriorate in the cold, wet weather. He led them to her body in the woods about half a mile away. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004]
- Nov 1, 2012: body recovered that morning. White arraigned on first-degree murder and open murder; held without bail. [10-09-S001]
- **Mar 27, 2013**: pleaded guilty to second-degree murder as a habitual offender. [10-09-S002]
- **Apr 18, 2013**: sentenced to **56 years 3 months to 85 years** by Chief Judge Paul H. Chamberlain. [10-09-S002, 10-09-S008]
- **Aug 28, 2013**: White died in his cell at the Michigan Reformatory in Ionia. MDOC reported it as suicide. [10-09-S008]

# Evidence reveal

- The case was solved by **confession**: White led investigators to her body (sheriff and court filing). [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004]
- By his own account to police, he struck her with a mallet and strangled her with a zip tie. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S010]
- His record: a 1981 knife attack on a teenage girl, and a 1995 manslaughter. Released in 2007. [10-09-S003, 10-09-S004]

# Ending

- At sentencing, her mother said: "She was not yours to take. How dare you." [10-09-S006]
- Her sister Deborah called for a public **violent-offender registry**, saying Rebekah might be alive if the family had known White's history. [10-09-S006]
- Four months after sentencing, White was dead. The case is closed. [10-09-S008]
