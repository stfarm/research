## D. Thumbnail concepts (dedicated covers; 10-09 brand standard)

1. **"Her last appointment"** — adult woman photographer (illustrative, not a likeness of Teresa) lowering a camera, wary glance toward an off-frame trailer, junkyard bokeh behind. White: `TERESA HALBACH`. Gold: `HER LAST PHOTO SHOOT WAS HALLOWEEN`. (S002.)
2. **"The bonfire"** — close on a man's silhouette lit orange by a large fire at night (no identifiable face). White: `MAKING A MURDERER`. Gold: `WITNESSES SAW A BONFIRE THAT NIGHT`. (S002.)
3. **"The same girl"** — old landline/office phone receiver in foreground, a woman's portrait-style face in soft focus behind (illustrative). Gold: `HE ASKED FOR "THE SAME GIRL"`. (S002 quote.)

Rules: do not depict Avery or Dassey as illustrative look-alikes; no remains; no RAV4 "planted" imagery.
