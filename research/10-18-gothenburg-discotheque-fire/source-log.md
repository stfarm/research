Access dates: 2026-10-01 unless noted. "Fetched" means the page text was retrieved and read. "Snippet" means only search-result excerpts were seen.

Note: The 2001 Statens haverikommission (SHK) report and the court judgments were not read in full online. Official findings come from the government commission summary (SOU 2000:113), SHK findings as summarised by Swedish sources, and NFPA's investigation summary. CNN's 1998 report returned HTTP 451.
