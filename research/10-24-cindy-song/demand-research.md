## Interpretation

- **Case-specific, multi-year, multi-language demand.** Six long-form videos between 104K and 294K, from 2018 to 2026, in English, Hungarian and Portuguese.
- **Recent interest:** a July 29, 2026 upload has 149,734 views in ~2.5 months on a 128K-subscriber channel (more views than subscribers). A Shorts upload on Sept 28, 2026 reached 1,356 on a 145-subscriber channel.
- **Evergreen/seasonal:** most uploads cluster in late October.
- **Short-form:** small (≤3.5K). Long-form is where demand is proven.
- Compared with the truck attack it replaces (news clips only; no creator coverage found), Cindy Song shows clear creator demand.

## Why it earned 10-24

Genuine Halloween-night setting (costume, Halloween club party), a tight and well-sourced timeline, an eerie "made it home" puzzle, and stronger case-specific demand than the case it replaces. Labelled: **unresolved disappearance; no crime established.**

## Title / curiosity angles (accurate)

- "She Made It Home After Halloween. Then She Vanished."
- "Her Phone Was Still in the Apartment. She Wasn't."
- "The Halloween Bunny Costume Was Never Found"

Avoid: "kidnapped", "murdered", "serial killer took her", "Playboy bunny".
